# Türkiye: legal, regulatory and institutional framework for retail-banking customer support (as of 10 October 2026)

Researcher 02. The jurisdiction is Türkiye only. All texts were checked between 9 and 10 October 2026. Consolidated laws and regulations come from mevzuat.gov.tr; originals and amendments come from resmigazete.gov.tr (Resmî Gazete, RG). Board decisions come from bddk.org.tr, and published rates/limits from tcmb.gov.tr, tmsf.org.tr, ticaret.gov.tr, tbb.org.tr, kvkk.gov.tr and masak.hmb.gov.tr.

**Tag legend** (used on every rule):
- **[1-Law]**: law, regulation, communiqué or binding board decision.
- **[1-Case]**: court decision.
- **[2-Practice]**: common industry practice.
- **[3-Bank]**: an individual bank's policy.
- **[4-Rec]**: recommended service practice for the playbook.
- **[5-Verify]**: an assumption or secondary-source claim that needs verification.

**Corrections to the brief that matter for the playbook:**
1. The current financial-consumer fee rules are a TCMB *Tebliğ* (communiqué No. 2020/7, RG 7 Mar 2020 No. 31061), not a *Yönetmelik*. The old BDDK regulation of the same name (RG 3 Oct 2014) was repealed on 7 Mar 2020 ([RG index 7 Mar 2020](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2020/03/20200307.htm)).
2. Law 6493 itself has no consumer-liability, refund or IBAN-error articles. Those rules sit in the TCMB regulation of 2021 (RG 1 Dec 2021 No. 31676), and its art. 83 extends most of them to banks.
3. The TBB "Müşteri Şikayetleri Hakem Heyeti" is now called the **Bireysel Müşteri Hakem Heyeti** (BMHH).
4. Some official titles differ from the brief:
   - *Sır Niteliğindeki Bilgilerin Paylaşılması Hakkında Yönetmelik*
   - *Bankalarca Kullanılacak Uzaktan Kimlik Tespiti Yöntemlerine ve Elektronik Ortamda Sözleşme İlişkisinin Kurulmasına İlişkin Yönetmelik*
5. Two BDDK regulations not named in the brief are central to support work:
   - *Banka Çağrı Merkezlerinin Hizmet Seviyesinin ve Kalitesinin Belirlenmesine İlişkin Yönetmelik* (RG 20 May 2020 No. 31132): call-center service levels, a human-agent option and a direct-to-agent option for customers aged 60+.
   - *Bankacılık Hizmetlerinin Erişilebilirliğine Dair Yönetmelik* (RG 18 Jun 2016 No. 29746): accessibility.

## 1. Authorities: roles and consumer-facing functions

### Takeaway
Supervision is split three ways:
- **BDDK** supervises banks, card issuers and asset-management companies (VYŞ).
- **TCMB** runs payment systems (EFT/FAST), sets the maximum card and KMH rates, makes the fee rules for financial consumers, and supervises payment and e-money institutions.
- **Ministry of Trade** runs consumer law and the THH.

Data protection belongs to **KVKK** and AML to **MASAK**. Industry bodies run voluntary-but-binding arbitration: TBB (and TKBB for participation banks) through the BMHH, and the credit-risk infrastructure (TBB Risk Merkezi, served through KKB/Findeks). BDDK's own routing page tells consumers where each kind of complaint goes.

### Cited Findings
- **BDDK.**
  - Mandate under 5411 art. 93: protect depositors' rights and the effective functioning of the credit system, and issue regulations and board decisions [1-Law] ([5411 consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5411&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)).
  - Its 2026 consumer-credit board decisions cite 5411 art. 93 and 5464 arts. 9 and 14 as their legal basis [1-Law] ([Decision 11367](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1326)).
  - BDDK's routing rules [1-Law/official guidance] ([BDDK İletişim, accessed 10 Oct 2026](https://www.bddk.org.tr/iletisim)):
    - Refund claims on individual banking go to the arbitration boards: THH via e-Devlet, TBB, TKBB. Their decisions bind up to certain amounts; above those amounts the route is the consumer courts.
    - Complaints about interest, fees, charges and commissions go to **TCMB**.
    - Other complaints about supervised institutions go to **BDDK E-Şikayet**.
    - Complaints about BDDK itself go by petition or through **CİMER**.
  - BDDK E-Şikayet ([ebulten.bddk.org.tr/esikayet](https://ebulten.bddk.org.tr/esikayet/)) accepts complaints about banks and other financial institutions and forwards each complaint to the bank before assessing it. It advises complaining to the bank first. Insurance complaints go to SEDDK. The same service is on e-Devlet ([turkiye.gov.tr/bbdk-sikayet-basvuru-takibi](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/bbdk-sikayet-basvuru-takibi)).
- **TCMB.**
  - Operates EFT and FAST. TCMB payment systems run weekdays 08:30–17:30, and until 13:00 on half days. TCMB may set different hours for customer payments. FAST is TCMB-operated and runs 7/24 [1-Law/official] ([TCMB EFT/FAST page](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Temel+Faaliyetler/Odeme+Sistemleri/Turkiyedeki+Odeme+Sistemleri/Elektronik+Fon+Transfer+%28EFT%29+Sistemi)).
  - Sets maximum contractual and default card interest rates under 5464 art. 26, as amended by Law 7222 in 2020 [1-Law] ([5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5464&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)). KMH rates may not exceed TCMB's maximums under TCMB Communiqué 2020/3 [1-Law] ([TCMB rate table page](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Istatistikler/Bankacilik+Verileri/Kredi_Karti_Islemlerinde_Uygulanacak_Azami_Faiz_Oranlari)).
  - Issues the fee communiqué 2020/7, which rests on 6502 art. 4(3) [1-Law] ([RG 7 Mar 2020](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2020/03/20200307-9.htm)).
  - Regulates payment services under the 2021 regulation [1-Law] ([RG 1 Dec 2021](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2021/12/20211201-1.htm)).
  - Complaint portal: [esikayet.tcmb.gov.tr](https://esikayet.tcmb.gov.tr/mbnesspfe-kullanici/).
  - e-Devlet service "Ödeme ve Elektronik Para Kuruluşu Hesap Sahipliği Sorgulama" lets a person see payment/e-money accounts opened in their name ([turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/tcmb-odeme-ve-elektronik-para-kurulusu-hesap-bilgisi)). This is useful for identity-misuse cases.
- **MASAK.** The FIU under the Ministry of Treasury and Finance ([masak.hmb.gov.tr](https://masak.hmb.gov.tr/)) [1-Law] ([5549](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5549&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)):
  - KYC duty: art. 3.
  - STR duty: art. 4(1).
  - Tipping-off ban: art. 4(2).
  - Penalty for breaching art. 4(2), 7 or 8: 1–3 years' imprisonment plus a judicial fine (art. 14).
  - The Minister may suspend or block a transaction for **7 business days** (art. 19/A).
- **KVKK.** Data-protection authority under Law 6698. Complaints go to the Board only after the controller application route is exhausted (art. 14(2)) [1-Law] ([6698](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6698&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)).
- **TMSF.** Deposit insurer (2026 cover: TL 1,200,000 per person per credit institution, including commercial deposits; foreign-branch and offshore deposits are excluded) and recipient of time-barred deposits (5411 art. 62) [1-Law/official] ([TMSF FAQ](https://www.tmsf.org.tr/tr/Tmsf/Finansman/mevduat.sss.guncel)). Details in section 5.
- **Ministry of Trade.** Consumer law (6502): THH, unfair commercial practices (art. 62), consumer courts and mandatory mediation (arts. 73, 73/A). THH applications go through e-Devlet/TÜBİS ([tuketicisikayeti.ticaret.gov.tr](https://tuketicisikayeti.ticaret.gov.tr)). Details in section 4.
- **TBB.** Hosts the **Risk Merkezi** (5411 Ek Madde 1), runs the **BMHH** (section 4), and coordinates ATM accessibility with TKBB (accessibility regulation arts. 6–7) [1-Law].
- **TKBB.** Runs its own BMHH ([TKBB BMHH](https://tkbb.org.tr/sayfa/detay/mevzuat/bireysel-musteri-hakem-heyeti-209167); page not opened [5-Verify]). Jointly with TBB, it decides where card applications may be collected outside branches (5464 art. 8) [1-Law].
- **BKM.** BDDK granted BKM an operating license as a card system ("kartlı sistem") by Board Decision 6821 of 31 Mar 2016 [1-Law] ([BDDK lists](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/Liste/51)). No consumer complaint function was found [5-Verify].
- **KKB/Findeks.** Findeks forwards Risk Report objections to the reporting bank and says they are resolved within 24 hours. KKB states it has no right or discretion to delete or correct records [2-Practice/official FAQ] ([Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)). The Risk Merkezi report is also available on e-Devlet ([turkiye.gov.tr](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/turkiye-bankalar-birligi-risk-merkezi-raporu-basvurusu)).
- **BTK.** Two amending regulations took effect on **25 Jun 2026**: one to the *Elektronik Haberleşme Sektöründe Başvuru Sahibinin Kimliğinin Doğrulanma Süreci Hakkında Yönetmelik*, one to the consumer-rights regulation. Press reports describe electronic identity verification for subscriptions, periodic re-verification, restriction after 30 days and closure after 90 days for unverified lines, and per-person line caps. RG date and details: [5-Verify] (press: [Cumhuriyet](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/kimlik-dogrulama-kurallari-degisti-resmi-gazete-de-yayimlandi-telefon-ve-internet-aboneliklerinde-yeni-donem-2511185), [DijitalKarakol](https://dijitalkarakol.org/mobil-hatlarda-kimlik-dogrulama-donemi/)).
- **CİMER.** [cimer.gov.tr](https://www.cimer.gov.tr). BDDK lists it as a channel for information requests and complaints about BDDK's own activities ([BDDK İletişim](https://www.bddk.org.tr/iletisim)).
- **SPK.** Capital-markets regulator ([spk.gov.tr](https://spk.gov.tr)). Not researched in depth [5-Verify].

### Inferences
- The agent needs a routing matrix keyed on the remedy sought, not on the topic:
  - Money back → THH (under TL 186k) or BMHH.
  - Fee or interest legality → TCMB.
  - Service or conduct (no refund) → BDDK E-Şikayet.
  - Personal data → KVKK, after a written application to the bank.
  - Problems with a payment/e-money institution → TCMB.
- Regulators forward complaints to the bank first. The agent should therefore always offer to log a formal bank complaint and give a reference number.

### Gaps
- SPK's consumer complaint route and its overlap with bank branches selling investment products were not verified.
- BKM's consumer-facing role (e.g., TROY dispute rules) was not researched.
- The exact RG date of the BTK 2026 amendments was not captured.

## 2. Customer-service-relevant provisions of the primary laws

### Takeaway
Each core law has a small number of support-critical rules:
- **5411:** secrecy (art. 73) and dormancy (art. 62).
- **5464:** card complaints and liability (arts. 8, 9, 11, 12, 15, 16, 26, 32).
- **6493 + TCMB regulation:** payment refunds and wrong IBAN.
- **6502:** consumer credit and redress.
- **5549:** KYC/STR/tipping-off.
- **6698:** data rights.
- **TBK 77/82:** unjust enrichment for misdirected funds.
- **İİK 83/89 + 5510 art. 93 + 6183 art. 79:** garnishment.

### Cited Findings

**5411 Bankacılık Kanunu** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5411&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- **Art. 73, secrecy (*sır saklama*).**
  - Anyone who learns bank or customer secrets through their role may disclose them only to legally authorized bodies, and the duty survives the end of employment.
  - Added by Law 7222 (20 Feb 2020): customer data become a customer secret once the relationship starts. Except for statutory exceptions, customer-secret data **may not be shared with or transferred to third parties in Türkiye or abroad without a request or instruction from the customer, even if KVKK explicit consent has been obtained** (*müşterinin açık rızası alınsa dahi*).
  - Sharing under the exceptions must be limited to the stated purpose and proportionate.
  - BDDK may ban transfers abroad and require banks' information systems to be kept in Türkiye.
- **Art. 62, time-bar (*zamanaşımı*).** Deposits, participation funds, valuables and receivables not claimed within **10 years** of the holder's last request, transaction or written instruction become time-barred. If the holder cannot be reached, the bank publishes a notice and the amounts are recorded as income of the Fund (TMSF). Procedure: see section 3.
- **Ek Madde 1.** The Risk Merkezi at TBB. Members must report all requested customer data. Customers may obtain their own risk data for a fee. Disclosing Risk Merkezi secrets is punishable.

**6493** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6493&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5))
- The article list (arts. 1–43) covers payment systems, licensing, supervision and penalties. There are no articles on unauthorized transactions, refunds or wrong identifiers; those are in the TCMB regulation (section 3) [1-Law].

**5464 Banka Kartları ve Kredi Kartları Kanunu** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5464&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- **Art. 8:**
  - No card may be issued to anyone who did not request it or sign a contract.
  - If the minimum payment is unpaid within 3 months after the due date and still unpaid within 1 month of the issuer's notice, the issuer **cancels** the person's credit cards. No new card may be issued until the whole debt is paid.
  - Issuers must keep the notification, request, complaint and objection system **open without interruption** (*kesintisiz*).
  - On request, issuers must provide transaction records within 30 days (60 days for foreign transactions).
  - Issuers must protect PINs and passwords.
- **Art. 9:**
  - Limits are set by assessing income, other issuers' limits, scoring and KYC.
  - **No limit increase unless the cardholder requests it.**
  - Spending over the limit may only attract contractual interest.
  - Total limit for the first year: 2× monthly net income; second year: 4×.
- **Art. 11 (complaints and objections, *şikâyet ve itirazlar*):**
  - Issuers must answer complaints **within 20 days**, by the cardholder's application method and with reasons.
  - Statement objections must be filed **within 10 days of the due date**, stating the contested items.
  - An unobjected statement becomes final, but general court rights remain.
- **Art. 12 (misuse):**
  - For a lost or stolen card or its data, the holder bears losses from unlawful use in the **24 hours before notification, capped at TL 150** (text: "yüzelli Yeni Türk Lirası").
  - The cap does not apply where the holder was grossly negligent, acted intentionally, or failed to notify.
  - The issuer must offer insurance for that TL 150 if the holder requests it and pays the premium.
  - Art. 47 lets BDDK uprate monetary amounts by PPI. No uprating decision was found [5-Verify].
- **Art. 15:** The cardholder is **not liable** for unlawful remote purchases where no signed slip exists (telephone, order form or other communications), i.e. card-not-present.
- **Art. 16:** The cardholder must protect the card and PIN and notify the issuer **immediately** of loss, theft or any unauthorized transaction. Address changes must be notified within 15 days.
- **Art. 26:**
  - Contractual interest applies if at least the minimum is paid; default interest applies below the minimum.
  - **No compound interest**, even in default.
  - At least 10 days between statement date and due date.
  - TCMB sets the maximum rates.
- **Art. 32:**
  - In remote (card-not-present) disputes, the **burden of proof is on the merchant**.
  - Between issuer and cardholder, the **issuer** must prove the transaction was recorded correctly with no technical fault.
  - Telephone notifications must go to recorded call centers.
  - Recordings must be kept **1 year**, or until a dispute is resolved.
- **Art. 33:** Duty of prudence and care (*basiret ve özen*).

**6502 Tüketicinin Korunması Hakkında Kanun** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6502&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- **Art. 24, withdrawal from a consumer credit (*cayma*):**
  - 14 days, with no reason needed and no penalty.
  - The lender must prove the consumer was informed of the right.
  - Since Law 7392 (2022), full early repayment within the period counts as withdrawal without a separate notice.
  - The consumer repays principal plus accrued contractual interest within 30 days of the notice.
- **Art. 27, early repayment:** Partial or full early repayment is allowed, and the lender must reduce interest and cost elements accordingly.
- **Art. 28, default and acceleration (*temerrüt/muacceliyet*):** The lender may call in the whole debt only if:
  - the lender has performed fully;
  - the consumer is in default on **at least two consecutive installments**;
  - the lender has given **at least 30 days'** acceleration warning.
  Interest, commissions and similar costs are excluded when computing the accelerated installments.
- **Art. 30:** Linked credit (*bağlı kredi*). If the goods or service are not delivered, the lender is jointly liable, within limits.
- **Art. 48:** Distance contracts, with a 14-day withdrawal right. Financial-services specifics are in a separate Ministry regulation that was not verified [5-Verify].
- **Art. 62:** Unfair commercial practices, including deceptive or **aggressive** ones, are banned. The trader must prove a practice is not unfair.
- **Arts. 68–70, consumer arbitration committees (THH):**
  - THH application is **mandatory** below a threshold (base TL 30,000 from 2022, revalued each January). THH may not be used above it.
  - Decisions **bind** both parties and are enforced as court judgments.
  - Either party may object to the consumer court **within two weeks** of notification; the objection does not stay enforcement unless the judge orders it.
- **Art. 73:** Consumer courts. Suits by consumers are **exempt from court fees** (art. 73(2)).
- **Art. 73/A:** **Mandatory pre-suit mediation** (added by Law 7251, 2020). Exceptions: THH-scope disputes, objections to THH decisions, and certain others. In listed cases the consumer's mediation fee is paid by the Ministry of Justice.

**5549 Suç Gelirlerinin Aklanmasının Önlenmesi Hakkında Kanun** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5549&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- Art. 3: identify customers before transactions.
- Art. 4(1): report suspicious transactions, including attempted ones.
- **Art. 4(2): the institution may not tell anyone, including the parties to the transaction, that an STR was filed.** The only exceptions are inspectors and courts.
- Art. 14: 1–3 years' imprisonment.
- Art. 19/A: suspension for 7 business days.

**6698 KVKK** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6698&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- **Art. 11 rights:**
  - (a)–(c) to learn whether, why and how data is processed;
  - (ç) to know the third-party recipients;
  - (d) correction;
  - (e) erasure;
  - (f) notification of corrections and erasures to recipients;
  - **(g) to object to an adverse outcome produced exclusively by automated analysis**;
  - (ğ) compensation.
- **Art. 13:** The application is made in writing or by Board-approved methods. The controller decides **as soon as possible and at the latest within 30 days, free of charge**; a Board-tariff fee is allowed only for extra cost. The reply must be in writing or electronic.
- **Art. 14:** Complaint to the Board within 30 days of the reply, and in any case within 60 days of the application. The controller route must be used first.
- **Art. 9 (rewritten by Law 7499, 2 Mar 2024):** Cross-border transfers are allowed:
  - on an adequacy decision; or
  - with appropriate safeguards: binding corporate rules, **standard contracts (to be notified to the Authority within 5 business days)**, or undertakings; or
  - occasionally, on listed grounds such as informed explicit consent or contract necessity.
  The effective date of 1 Jun 2024 is [5-Verify] (not read in the consolidated text).

**Türk Borçlar Kanunu (6098)** ([consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6098&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]
- Art. 77: whoever is enriched without just cause must return the enrichment (*sebepsiz zenginleşme*).
- **Art. 82:** the claim is time-barred **2 years** from learning of the right to reclaim, and **10 years** from the enrichment in any case.

**Garnishment (*haciz*)** [1-Law]
- **İİK art. 83:** salaries and pensions may be garnished after deducting what the debtor and family need, but "the garnished amount cannot be less than one quarter" ([İİK](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=2004&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=3)). In practice, a quarter of salary for ordinary debts [2-Practice, 5-Verify].
- **İİK art. 83/a:** prior agreements allowing garnishment of exempt assets are void.
- **İİK art. 89:** a garnishment notice (*haciz ihbarnamesi*) to a third party such as a bank. The third party has **7 days** to object.
- **5510 art. 93:** SGK pensions and benefits cannot be assigned and **cannot be garnished**, except for SGK claims and alimony. Enforcement offices reject requests unless the debtor consents ([5510](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5510&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)).
- **6183 art. 79:** tax-office garnishment notices to banks may be served **electronically (e-haciz)**, and banks may answer electronically ([6183](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=6183&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=3)).

**TCK 158/4 (Law 7589, accepted 16 Jul 2026, RG 31 Jul 2026 No. 33326)** [1-Law] ([RG](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/07/20260731-1.htm))
- Where a person's participation in fraud or qualified fraud consists only of giving their own or another's bank/credit card or other payment instrument, or the information or tools needed to use an account at a bank, brokerage, payment-service provider or crypto-asset provider, to another person for unjust benefit, **the penalty is reduced by half**.
- This is a sentencing reduction for accomplices, **not a standalone offense**. Earlier press-reported drafts proposed a separate 1–3-year crime ([Milliyet](https://uzmanpara.milliyet.com.tr/uzmanpara/son-dakika-12-yargi-paketine-ek-geliyor-iban-kiralama-tek-basina-suc-sayilacak-7611317)). The final text differs.

### Inferences
- Secrecy (art. 73) is stricter than KVKK. Verbal consent from a caller claiming to be family is never enough. The agent must authenticate the customer and act only on the customer's own instruction.
- **Customer phrasings (Turkish):**
  - "Kartım çalındı, benim yapmadığım harcamalar var" (card stolen, transactions I didn't make). Apply the 5464 rules: immediate block, the TL 150 / 24h exposure, and a CNP exemption under art. 15.
  - "Ekstremdeki işleme itiraz etmek istiyorum" (I want to dispute a statement item). Note the 10-day window and the 20-day reply deadline.
  - "Maaşıma/emekli maaşıma haciz geldi" (my salary/pension has been garnished). Explain the general protections, then route to the legal or garnishment team. Do not interpret the enforcement file.
  - "IBAN'ımı arkadaşıma kullandırdım" (I let a friend use my IBAN). Do not give legal advice. Note the TCK 158/4 risk and refer the customer to legal counsel [4-Rec].

### Gaps
- The details of the Tüketici Kredisi Sözleşmeleri Yönetmeliği and the distance financial-services regulation were not opened.
- Whether BDDK ever uprated the TL 150 card cap under 5464 art. 47 is not known.

## 3. Secondary regulations and their practical effects

### Takeaway
The operative support rules sit in secondary legislation:
- the card regulation (closures, statements, minimum payment);
- the TCMB payment regulation (refunds, wrong IBAN, 20-day complaints);
- the fee communiqué (consent, 30-day notice, free items);
- BSEBY and BDDK Circular 2023/1 (authentication and telephone banking);
- the call-center regulation (service levels, human option, 60+ priority);
- the remote-ID regulation;
- the secrecy-sharing and outsourcing regulations (which shape vendor and AI use);
- the accessibility regulation.

### Inferences
- The AI agent must operate under BSEBY art. 40 if voice-based. It must not access customer data before 2FA, must never collect PINs or OTPs itself, and must always offer a path to a human. These are explicit legal duties, not just good practice.

## 4. Complaint and redress path for a bank customer

### Takeaway
The path runs in five steps:
1. The bank's internal complaint (20 days for cards and payments; 30 days for KVKK).
2. The right regulator route: BDDK E-Şikayet for conduct, TCMB for fees, interest and payment/e-money institutions, KVKK for data.
3. A binding redress body:
   - **THH** is mandatory under **TL 186,000 (2026)**. It is filed via TÜBİS, is binding, and can be objected to within 2 weeks.
   - **TBB/TKBB BMHH** is free and binding on the bank up to **TL 51,158 (2026)**.
4. Mandatory mediation, then consumer court (court-fee exempt), for larger disputes.
5. **CİMER** as a general petition channel.

### Cited Findings
- **Bank first.** Reply deadlines: cards 20 days ([5464 art. 11](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=5464&MevzuatTur=1&MevzuatTertip=5)); payment services 20 days (TCMB regulation art. 74(1)); data requests 30 days (KVKK art. 13). BMHH requires proof of the bank complaint (the bank's answer or a dated receipt) and a 30-day bank reply period (20 days for cards, extendable by 10) ([TBB FAQ](https://www.tbb.org.tr/finansal-tuketici/sikca-sorulan-sorular)) [1-Law/official scheme rule].
- **BDDK E-Şikayet.** For conduct complaints with no refund sought; forwards to the bank first ([ebulten.bddk.org.tr/esikayet](https://ebulten.bddk.org.tr/esikayet/); [e-Devlet](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/bbdk-sikayet-basvuru-takibi)).
- **TCMB.**
  - Handles interest, fee, charge and commission complaints about banking products, and complaints about payment/e-money institutions ([BDDK routing](https://www.bddk.org.tr/iletisim); [TCMB Şikayet Yönetim Sistemi](https://esikayet.tcmb.gov.tr/mbnesspfe-kullanici/)).
  - The portal's notice says court action remains open if the bank does not resolve the issue.
  - No application fee was found [5-Verify].
- **THH (consumer arbitration committees).**
  - 2026: disputes **below TL 186,000** must go to an ilçe or il THH. At or above that amount, THH may not be used; the route is mandatory mediation, then the consumer court (or civil court of first instance where no consumer court exists).
  - Filing: in person, through a lawyer, by post, or on e-Devlet/TÜBİS. No oral applications.
  - Decisions are binding and enforceable. Objection goes to the consumer court within two weeks of notification.
  - Sources: [Ticaret Bakanlığı](https://ticaret.gov.tr/tuketici/tuketici-hakem-heyetleri/tuketici-hakem-heyetlerine-iliskin-bilgilendirme-metni); threshold communiqué RG 23 Dec 2025 ([RG](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2025/12/20251223-5.htm)); base rule 6502 arts. 68–70 [1-Law].
- **TBB Bireysel Müşteri Hakem Heyeti.**
  - Natural persons only, individual (non-commercial) banking only.
  - The event must be **≤ 2 years old**.
  - The service is **free**.
  - Decisions **bind the bank up to TL 51,158 (valid from 1 Jan 2026; CPI-indexed each January)**. A decision in the bank's favor creates no enforcement right for the bank.
  - Four sub-boards: cards; consumer loans; insurance; other products.
  - Each sub-board: 2 BDDK members + 3 bank representatives.
  - Excluded: credit or pricing policy, transactions that have not yet occurred, complaints about staff, general practices, **material or moral damages**, and matters already before a court or THH. A later THH filing ends the BMHH's jurisdiction.
  - Filing: form by email (tbbhh@tbb.org.tr) or post. Tracking on e-Devlet. In-person applications are refused.
  - Sources: [TBB BMHH](https://www.tbb.org.tr/faaliyetler/bireysel-musteri-hakem-heyeti); [FAQ](https://www.tbb.org.tr/finansal-tuketici/sikca-sorulan-sorular); [e-Devlet](https://www.turkiye.gov.tr/tbb-bireysel-musteri-hakem-heyeti) [1-Law/official scheme rules].
- **TKBB BMHH** for participation banks ([link](https://tkbb.org.tr/sayfa/detay/mevzuat/bireysel-musteri-hakem-heyeti-209167)). Limits not verified [5-Verify].
- **Consumer courts.** Consumer suits are exempt from court fees (6502 art. 73(2)). Mandatory mediation comes first, except for THH-scope disputes and objections to THH decisions (art. 73/A) [1-Law].
- **KVKK route.**
  1. Written application to the bank (KVKK application communiqué art. 5).
  2. Reply within 30 days, in writing or electronically. **Oral or phone replies are not accepted as the legal reply** ([KVKK notice, 1 Oct 2026](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/9024/ilgili-kisilerin-basvurularina-verilecek-cevabin-bildirim-usulune-iliskin-kamuoyu-duyurusu)).
  3. Complaint to the Board within 30 / 60 days.
  4. The Board has warned a bank that ignored a valid postal application, and told it to use traceable delivery such as KEP, e-mail or APS rather than ordinary post (Decision 2022/863, [KVKK](https://kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7582/2022-863)) [1-Law].
- **CİMER.** [cimer.gov.tr](https://www.cimer.gov.tr): general petitions to the Presidency. BDDK lists it for petitions about BDDK itself.

### Inferences
- **Script logic [4-Rec]:**
  1. Log a formal complaint and give a reference number.
  2. State the bank's reply deadline: 20 days for card or payment matters; otherwise the bank's internal SLA [3-Bank].
  3. If the customer is unsatisfied and wants money back:
     - under TL 51,158 → BMHH (free, binding on the bank) or THH;
     - under TL 186,000 → THH (mandatory if they want a binding public route);
     - above → mediation, then court.
  4. Fee or interest legality → TCMB.
  5. Conduct or security grievance → BDDK E-Şikayet.
  6. Data → KVKK (written application first).
- **Customer phrasings (Turkish):**
  - "Şikâyetimi nereye iletebilirim?" (where can I complain?)
  - "BDDK'ya şikâyet edeceğim" (I'll complain to BDDK)
  - "Hakem heyetine başvurmak istiyorum" (I want to apply to the arbitration board)
  - "Kişisel verilerimin silinmesini istiyorum" (I want my personal data deleted). This is a KVKK art. 11 request: send it to the written channel; do not resolve it orally.

## 6. Anti-fraud and account-misuse measures (2023–2026); refund position and case law

### Takeaway
Türkiye's anti-fraud framework rests on bank-side technical duties:
- no SMS OTP once the app is activated;
- operator-integrated **SIM-swap detection** with a 90-day block;
- dynamic linking;
- transaction monitoring;
- fraud reporting first in the IVR;
- agents barred from handling OTPs or PINs.

It is supplemented by **TCK 158/4 (2026)** on account renting, MASAK reporting and BTK's 2026 line-verification rules. On refunds:
- For non-card payments the regulations cap customer liability at **TL 250** (TL 150 for cards) and put the **burden of proof on the bank**.
- Gross negligence or intent shifts the full loss to the customer.
- The Yargıtay consistently treats banks as "trust institutions" liable **even for slight fault**, and reduces compensation for customer fault only if the bank proves it.

### Cited Findings
- **Account renting.** TCK 158/4 (Law 7589, RG 31 Jul 2026) halves the penalty for fraud accomplices whose role was handing over accounts or payment tools ([RG](https://www.resmigazete.gov.tr/eskiler/2026/07/20260731-1.htm)) [1-Law]. Account holders thus remain criminally exposed as accomplices, and the reduced penalty is still a penalty.
- **SIM-swap control.** BSEBY art. 34(8): operator integration to detect SIM changes and porting before SMS OTP; a 90-day ban on SIM-based factors unless the change is confirmed [1-Law]. BTK's 2026 amendments (effective 25 Jun 2026) tighten subscriber identity verification [5-Verify details] ([press](https://www.cumhuriyet.com.tr/turkiye/kimlik-dogrulama-kurallari-degisti-resmi-gazete-de-yayimlandi-telefon-ve-internet-aboneliklerinde-yeni-donem-2511185)).
- **Authentication.** BSEBY art. 34(7) (no SMS OTP after app activation), art. 34(10) (no ID-card data or mother's maiden name), art. 34(12) (customer limits and safe-payee lists) and BDDK Circular 2023/1 (codes bound to amount and payee; device-bound keys) [1-Law].
- **Monitoring and warnings.** BSEBY art. 36 (anomaly monitoring; prompt customer warning) and art. 40(4) (check call forwarding before calling the customer) [1-Law].
- **Mule prevention at onboarding.** In remote ID, the representative judges whether the applicant acts of their own will (remote-ID regulation art. 5(4)) [1-Law].
- **Night-time measure (Nov 2025, press).** BDDK reportedly told banks to require **NFC verification of the chip ID card** for credit-card limit increases and cash advances between **22:00 and 06:00**. No BDDK instrument was cited ([ShiftDelete, 12 Nov 2025](https://shiftdelete.net/bddkdan-kredi-karti-kisitlamasi-limit-ve-nakit-avans-icin-nfc-sarti-geldi)) [5-Verify].
- **AML.** STR and the tipping-off ban; MASAK may suspend transactions for 7 business days (5549 arts. 4 and 19/A) [1-Law].
- **Refund rules (regulatory):** TCMB regulation art. 54 for non-card payments, 5464 arts. 12, 15 and 32 for cards, and BSEBY art. 34(3), (8) on the burden of proof (details in sections 2 and 3) [1-Law].
- **Case law (Yargıtay)** [1-Case], via [Sert Sütçü, Ankara Barosu Dergisi 2025/4 (peer-reviewed)](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/4814684):
  - **YHGK 2013/2425 E., 2015/2022 K. (30 Sep 2015):** banks are trust institutions under intense state oversight and attract special public trust.
  - **YHGK 2017/153 E., 2019/916 K. (19 Sep 2019); Y. 11. HD 2018/3341 E., 2019/4515 K. (17 Jun 2019); Y. 11. HD 2016/11264 E., 2018/3306 K. (8 May 2018):** banks owe an **objective duty of care** and are **liable even for slight fault** (*hafif kusur*).
  - **YHGK 2012/11-550 E., 2012/820 K. (21 Nov 2012):** the bank may seek a reduction **only if it proves** the depositor's contributory fault (*müterafik kusur*).
  - **Y. 11. HD 2009/1065 E., 2010/6921 K. (15 Jun 2010):** the lower court had assigned 2/3 of the fault to the customer for failing to protect a password. Yargıtay held the bank bore the burden and had not proved the password was compromised through the customer's fault, so no customer fault applied.
  - The article also quotes Yargıtay reasoning that in internet banking the customer's only duty is to protect the password, and a breach of it can amount only to contributory fault.
  - Separately, a summary of YHGK 2017/11-153 E., 2019/916 K. (forged-signature branch withdrawal) records the reversal of a 75/25 apportionment to full bank liability ([law-firm summary](https://karamercanhukuk.com/yargitay-karari/somut-olayda-davaci-zarar-goren-banka-musterisinin-muterafik-kusurunun-bulunmadigi-kabul-edilmelidir)) [1-Case via secondary].
  - A search snippet attributes to **YHGK 2017/2224 E., 2018/1753 K.** the statement that banks' duty of care "increases further" once they offer internet banking. The full text was behind a login [5-Verify].
- **Counter-trend (first-instance or appeal level).** The same article reproduces a lower-court finding that transactions made **from the customer's own registered device** after the customer enabled "remember me" showed the **customer's gross negligence**, with no bank liability [1-Case, court level 5-Verify]. Some lower courts split the loss 50/50 when neither party's fault is clear (article reproduction). Yargıtay outcomes vary with the evidence.

### Inferences
- **Support stance [4-Rec]:**
  - Never prejudge liability ("you shared your code, so it's your fault").
  - Block the instrument immediately.
  - Record the time of notification: it ends customer exposure under TCMB regulation art. 54 and 5464 art. 12.
  - Open a dispute and quote the 20-day reply deadline.
  - Inform the customer of the BMHH, THH and court options.
  - Advise filing a police or prosecutor complaint for fraud [2-Practice].
- The agent **must not** ask for or accept PINs, passwords, OTPs or CVV, even when the customer offers them. BSEBY art. 40(3) requires that such entry go to automated systems without agent access.
- **Customer phrasings (Turkish):**
  - "Bankadan aradılar, kodu söyledim" (they called from the bank and I gave the code). Treat as a social-engineering case: block, record, open a dispute. Do not prejudge gross negligence.
  - "SIM kartım değişmiş, hesabımdan para gitmiş" (my SIM was changed and money left my account). Trigger the SIM-swap check under BSEBY art. 34(8).
  - "Hesabıma bloke konuldu, neden?" (why was a block put on my account?). If MASAK, STR or a suspension is involved, use neutral wording. **Do not disclose an STR** [1-Law].

### Gaps
- No public BDDK instrument was found for "new-device or new-customer transaction limits" or the night-time NFC rule (press only).
- No verified official statistics on fraud-refund outcomes.
- A primary Yargıtay database text for the 2018 internet-banking HGK decision could not be accessed.

## 7. Debt-collection contact rules, credit-bureau records and NPL transfers

### Takeaway
- **Contact with third parties.** KVKK has fined banks and collectors for contacting a debtor's relatives or workplace about the debt. **Even a call-back message that reveals the bank relationship to a relative has been held unlawful.**
- **Asset-management companies (VYŞ).** Since the 2025 amendments, the VYŞ regulation sets hard limits for collecting from consumers: weekdays **09:00–17:00**, **≤ 3 calls and 1 message a day**, **≥ 5 business days** between contacts once the debtor has been reached, no third-party contact, written notices.
- **Banks' own collections.** No equally explicit time window was found in BDDK rules for banks collecting themselves. The call-center regulation's 09:00–20:00 / no-Sunday limit applies to **sales** calls. Banks' own collections remain subject to KVKK, 5411 art. 73 and 6502 art. 62's ban on aggressive practices.

### Cited Findings
- **KVKK 2021/79 (3 Feb 2021).** A bank called the cardholder's sister and father, using numbers obtained through Risk Merkezi, about a credit-card debt. The call disclosed the bank relationship even though only a call-back request was left. Held unlawful (no art. 5 legal basis); breach of art. 12(1)(a); administrative fine ([KVKK](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7111/2021-79)) [1-Law].
- **KVKK 2023/78 (19 Jan 2023).** Debt SMS sent to company numbers of a firm in which the debtor was a partner. The **creditor (controller)** was fined **TL 85,000** for inaccurate contact data and breach of data security. The law-firm processor was not sanctioned ([KVKK](https://kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7596/2023-78)) [1-Law].
- Other KVKK decision titles show a pattern of related complaints: a bank, a VYŞ and lawyers pursuing a non-debtor (2021/424); a lawyer sharing a debt with the debtor's son (2022/1281) ([KVKK list](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7124/Kisisel-Verileri-Koruma-Kurulu-nun-Yeni-Yayinlanan-Karar-Ozetleri)). Full texts not read.
- **VYŞ regulation** (RG 14 Jul 2021 No. 31541; amended RG 27 Aug 2025 No. 32999; [consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=38797&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]:
  - **Art. 19:** besides NPLs, loans **more than 60 days overdue** may be transferred.
  - **Art. 20:**
    - Before collection starts, written notice to the debtor naming the original creditor and the VYŞ contacts, with no debt details.
    - At first contact, explain the source, amounts, ancillaries and possible legal steps clearly, and confirm in writing.
  - **Art. 21:**
    - Every settlement offer and each payment is confirmed in writing; the final confirmation states the debt is extinguished.
    - **No information to unauthorized third parties.**
    - Avoid methods damaging the debtor's reputation.
    - Verify identity before discussing the debt.
    - Deactivate numbers found to belong to third parties.
  - **Art. 24 (consumer debtors):**
    - Contact only on **weekdays 09:00–17:00 excluding public holidays**, and only with the debtor or authorized persons.
    - When the debtor is unreachable: **max 3 calls/day (across all lines) and 1 message**.
    - Once reached: **≥ 5 business days** before the next call, unless recorded consent.
    - **Do not call** people who deny the debt, refuse settlement and explicitly ask not to be called, unless the VYŞ holds concrete proof of the debt.
    - Run an accessible call center with a complaint log.
    - Meet the bank call-center service levels.
- **Banks.** The call-center regulation's time limits (art. 7) apply to **sales/marketing** outbound calls. The outsourcing regulation (art. 4(5)) allows outsourcing of overdue-debt notices [1-Law]. No bank-specific collection-hours rule was found [5-Verify].
- **6502 art. 62** bans aggressive commercial practices toward consumers, with the burden on the trader [1-Law].
- **Credit-bureau records.**
  - Reporting is mandatory for members (5411 Ek 1).
  - Sharing periods per Findeks (section 5).
  - **Objections** are filed through Findeks and forwarded to the bank. KKB cannot correct records itself.
  - A 2023 one-off "record amnesty" disregarded arrears due before 1 Oct 2022 and paid or restructured by 1 Jul 2023. The legal instrument number was not captured [5-Verify] ([Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)).
- **Card consequences of non-payment:** 5464 art. 8 (cancellation) and card regulation art. 22(6) (closures) [1-Law].
- **Acceleration of consumer loans:** 6502 art. 28 (2 consecutive installments + a 30-day warning) [1-Law].

### Inferences
- **Agent rules [4-Rec, grounded in 1-Law]:**
  - Never discuss or hint at a debt with anyone other than the authenticated debtor or an authorized representative. No messages to relatives, employers or company lines that reveal the bank relationship.
  - Treat the VYŞ art. 24 limits as the safe benchmark for bank collections.
  - Honor "beni aramayın" (don't call me) by recording the objection.
- After an NPL sale, tell the customer which VYŞ holds the debt, that the VYŞ must send written notice and confirm settlements in writing, and that the record stays visible at KKB until paid.
- **Customer phrasings (Turkish):**
  - "Borcumu neden anneme söylediniz?" (why did you tell my mother about my debt?). Log a complaint. Offer the KVKK art. 11/13 written route.
  - "Borcum varlık yönetim şirketine satılmış" (my debt was sold to an asset-management company). Explain the transfer and route to the VYŞ.
  - "Findeks notum hatalı" (my Findeks score/record is wrong). Correction must come from the reporting bank through the objection route.

### Gaps
- No Ticaret Bakanlığı-specific rule on time-of-day or frequency limits for collection calls was found.
- Reports of a 2026 BDDK or TBB collection code of conduct for banks were not found.

## 8. AI in banking customer service; accessibility obligations

### Takeaway
No horizontal AI statute was found in force as of October 2026. AI use in bank support is governed by existing law:
- KVKK, including art. 11(g) (the right to object to adverse automated-only outcomes) and art. 9 (cross-border transfers);
- 5411 art. 73 and the secrecy-sharing regulation (customer secrets reach vendors only through permitted channels);
- the outsourcing regulation (credit decisions cannot be outsourced);
- BSEBY art. 40 and the call-center regulation (authentication, a human-agent option, no call-time caps, QA scoring).

KVKK has issued generative-AI guidance (Nov 2025, Mar 2026). Accessibility duties are explicit and enforceable under BDDK's 2016 regulation.

### Cited Findings
- **KVKK.**
  - Art. 11(g): right to object to adverse results of solely automated analysis [1-Law].
  - "Üretken Yapay Zekâ ve Kişisel Verilerin Korunması Rehberi (15 Soruda)", published **24 Nov 2025** ([KVKK](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8547/uretken-yapay-zeka-ve-kisisel-verilerin-korunmasi-rehberi-15-soruda)).
  - "İş Yerlerinde Üretken Yapay Zekâ Araçlarının Kullanımı", **5 Mar 2026**, on third-party public generative-AI tools at work ([KVKK](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8674/is-yerlerinde-uretken-yapay-zeka-araclarinin-kullanimi)).
  - These are guidance documents, not binding [1-Law, guidance]. Their detailed recommendations were not extracted [5-Verify].
- **Cross-border data.** KVKK art. 9 (2024 regime) applies to foreign AI or cloud vendors. 5411 art. 73 lets BDDK ban transfers abroad and require systems to stay in Türkiye [1-Law].
- **Secrecy.** Customer secrets go to vendors only within the secrecy regulation's exceptions or on customer instruction. Consent cannot be made a precondition of service [1-Law].
- **Outsourcing.** Credit decisions are non-outsourceable. Call-center tasks are outsourceable within art. 4(5) [1-Law].
- **Telephone-banking rules apply regardless of human or automated handling:** 2FA before data access; no agent access to PIN or OTP; a human-agent option; IVR announcement caps (BSEBY art. 40). QA metrics and the 60+ direct-to-human option (call-center regulation) [1-Law].
- **Policy status.**
  - **Türkiye AI Action Plan 2026–2030** issued by Presidential Circular 2026/9, RG 18 Aug 2026 No. 33344 ([summary](https://www.erdem-erdem.av.tr/bilgi-bankasi/turkiye-yapay-zeka-eylem-plani-2026-2030-genelgesi-resmi-gazetede-yayimlandi)) [secondary; 5-Verify RG].
  - TBMM AI Research Commission report (S. Sayısı 260, 30 Mar 2026) ([SETA analysis](https://media.setav.org/tr/dosya/2026/06/turk-yapay-zeka-kanununa-dogru-tbmm-raporunun-hukuki-degerlendirmesi.pdf)).
  - A 2026 bill would add rules on AI-generated content to KVKK ([law-firm note](https://npartners.com.tr/kisisel-verilerin-korunmasi-ve-yapay-zek-2026-yilinda-tbmmye-sunulan-yeni-kanun-teklifi-ne-getiriyor/)).
  - No enacted AI law was found [5-Verify].
  - No BDDK AI-specific guidance was found [5-Verify].
- **Accessibility.** *Bankacılık Hizmetlerinin Erişilebilirliğine Dair Yönetmelik* (RG 18 Jun 2016 No. 29746; in force 1 Jan 2017; basis 5411 arts. 80, 93 and 5378 art. 7; [consolidated](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=38873&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)) [1-Law]:
  - Who counts as disabled:
    - customers presenting a disability report of **≥ 40%**;
    - **anyone aged 70+ automatically**, without notifying the bank.
  - Staff training.
  - **No extra fees** for accessibility services.
  - On request, contracts, statements and PIN/password communications in **Braille, audio or sign-language video**.
  - Disability data kept **confidential, least-privilege access**.
  - Free ATM basics for disabled customers registered with the bank, at any domestic ATM.
  - Talking ATMs; ATMs accessible to orthopedically disabled customers.
  - **Branch priority.**
  - **Remote sign-language video support** where no signing staff is available.
  - Accessible internet and mobile banking; **SMS OTPs readable by screen readers**.
  - Call centers:
    - password services also available outside the call center;
    - a **secure online chat or video-interpreter channel** for hearing-impaired customers, including **lost/stolen reporting**;
    - **authentication adapted to the disability**;
    - longer key-press time for visually impaired callers.
  - Remote ID: a third party may assist a disabled applicant (2023 amendment).
  - The 60+ direct-to-agent IVR option comes from the call-center regulation.

### Inferences
- An AI agent deployed by a Turkish bank should [4-Rec, grounded in 1-Law]:
  - authenticate before revealing anything;
  - never handle secrets (PIN, OTP, CVV, passwords);
  - always offer escalation to a human, immediately for 60+ customers and for lost/stolen/fraud;
  - not refuse card cancellation or redirect it elsewhere;
  - not cap conversation length;
  - log, for KVKK art. 11(g), any adverse automated decision, with a human-review path;
  - route KVKK requests to a written channel;
  - adapt its channels and authentication for customers who have declared a disability.
- Vendor and LLM hosting must be assessed under 5411 art. 73, the secrecy regulation, the outsourcing regulation and KVKK art. 9 before any customer data leaves the bank. Verify against the institution's legal and outsourcing approvals [5-Verify].

### Gaps
- The text of the KVKK generative-AI guide and the 2021 KVKK AI recommendations was not extracted.
- No TBB guidance for disabled customers was located beyond the regulation.
- BDDK cloud-specific rules were not reviewed in this pass.
