# 09 — Loans, credit, fees, statements and arrears/collections: what a bank support agent (including an AI phone agent) may explain, calculate and do. Türkiye is the primary jurisdiction, compared with the EU, UK and US. Status as of 2026-10-10.

**Tag legend (used on every statement):** [L-TR] / [L-EU] / [L-UK] / [L-US] = law or regulation in that jurisdiction · [I] = common industry practice · [B: Bank] = one named bank's or company's policy · [R] = recommended service practice (my synthesis) · [V] = assumption that needs verification. **PERIODIC** marks values that change regularly (rates, ratios, thresholds, maturities); re-check them before production use.
**Method:** I read BDDK Board decisions as the original PDFs, took TCMB maximum rates from the TCMB table (row valid 1/10/2026), and read mevzuat.gov.tr texts directly. All sources were accessed on 2026-10-10. Bank pages are used only as examples of practice. Şikayetvar is used only for customer wording and is marked as anecdotal.

## 1. Loan lifecycle touchpoints, application status, documents, credit decisions and rejections

### Takeaway
In Türkiye the agent can explain the process, application status, the document list, fees, and the customer's rights: pre-contract form, 14-day withdrawal, KVKK objection to purely automated outcomes. Only underwriting decides credit. The agent must never predict or promise approval, a limit or a rate. "Specific reasons for rejection" is a US legal duty (Reg B). From 20 Nov 2026 the EU adds rights to an explanation, human intervention and review (CCD2 Art. 18). I found no Turkish rule that requires lenders to give specific rejection reasons, so reason disclosure in Türkiye is bank policy [V].

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR] Before the contract is concluded, the lender must give the pre-contract information form (sözleşme öncesi bilgi formu) a reasonable time in advance (6502 art. 23). — [6502 TKHK](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf)
- [L-TR] The consumer has 14 days to withdraw from a consumer loan (cayma hakkı) with no reason and no penalty. They must repay the principal plus interest accrued to repayment within 30 days of the notice, otherwise the withdrawal is void. Only contractual interest and public or third-party costs may be charged. Since 2022 (Law 7392), repaying the loan in full within the window counts as withdrawal without a separate notice (art. 24). — [6502 TKHK](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf)
- [L-TR] Fixed-term consumer loans carry a fixed rate that cannot be changed to the consumer's detriment, and the contract terms cannot be worsened (arts. 25–26). If the contract omits the contractual rate, the effective annual rate or the total cost, the credit is interest-free. — [6502 TKHK](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf)
- [L-TR] Consumer loan contracts must state the effective annual interest rate (efektif yıllık faiz oranı), calculated by the Annex-1 formula. Fees for breaching the contract are excluded from it (Tüketici Kredisi Sözleşmeleri Yönetmeliği, TKS Yön., art. 14). — [TKS Yönetmeliği](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [B: Garanti BBVA] The "yıllık maliyet oranı" shown is the effective annual rate and excludes the insurance premium. The allocation fee is 0.5% of the loan, excluding BSMV. Status can be tracked under Başvurular > Kredi > "Devam Eden Kredi Başvuruları". Decisions usually take "a few business days" depending on amount, income, credit score and other criteria. Documents: ID with T.C. number; a payslip no older than 2 months or an employer income letter issued within the last month (SGK no., net salary, any garnishment); pension documents; tax plate and return for the self-employed; deed, lease and collection proof for rental income. A guarantor may be requested. — [Garanti BBVA Krediler SSS](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/krediler)
- [L-TR] The only fee allowed on consumer and housing loans is the allocation fee (tahsis ücreti), capped at 0.5% (binde beş) of principal. No "istihbarat", payment-plan-change or variable-plan fee may be charged under any name. Collateral and appraisal: third-party costs only. **No fee on rejected applications** except public or third-party costs (Tebliğ 2020/7 art. 10(1)–(3)). — [TCMB Tebliğ 2020/7](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR] Card limits must be based on: legal bans or impediments, economic and social status, average income, limits held at other issuers, model or scoring results, KYC, and data from the information-exchange company (5464 art. 9). Issuers cannot raise limits unless the cardholder asks (art. 9; Yön. art. 22(2)). — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf), [Kart Yönetmeliği](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=11180&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] BDDK decision 11367 (29.01.2026) on new cards and limit increases:
  - Total card limits may rest only on documented monthly or yearly income: at most 2× monthly income in the first year and 4× after that.
  - Cardholders with total limits of 400k–750k TL had limits cut by 50%, and those above 750k by 80%, of the lowest unused limit observed. Banks had to do this by 15.02.2026.
  - All limits must be aligned with income by **01.01.2027**.
  - The income used is reported to the TBB Risk Merkezi.
  — [BDDK 11367](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1326)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] Since 29.01.2026, a new KMH (overdraft) or a KMH increase is capped at 2× documented monthly average income. KMH limits for school-fee payments under bank–school agreements are exempt (BDDK 11365). — [BDDK 11365](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1324)
- [I-TR] Findeks says that applying for credit does not lower the Findeks score. Garanti describes the score as 1–1900 with risk bands from "çok iyi" to "yüksek riskli", and says a guarantor may be requested. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular); [Garanti BBVA](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/krediler)
- [L-TR] Under KVKK art. 11(1)(g), a data subject may **object to an adverse result produced solely by automated analysis**. They may also request correction (d). The controller must answer within 30 days, free of charge (art. 13(2)). — [KVKK 6698](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6698.pdf)
- [L-EU] CCD2 (Directive (EU) 2023/2225):
  - Art. 18(8): where creditworthiness assessment uses automated processing, the consumer may obtain human intervention, meaning a "clear and comprehensible explanation" (including the logic and risks), the chance to express their view, and a review of the assessment and decision.
  - Art. 18(9): rejected applicants must be told "without delay", referred to debt advice where relevant, and told about automated assessment and how to contest it.
  - Art. 18(11): the assessment must not rest exclusively on credit history.
  - Member States had to transpose by 20 Nov 2025 and **apply from 20 Nov 2026**.
  — [CCD2, EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:32023L2225)
- [L-EU] In SCHUFA (C-634/21, 7 Dec 2023), the CJEU held that credit scoring is an "automated individual decision" under the GDPR when lenders give it a determining role in granting credit. — [CJEU press release 186/23](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2023-12/cp230186en.pdf)
- [L-US] Reg B §1002.9:
  - Notice is due within 30 days of a completed application.
  - It must give either a statement of **specific** principal reasons, or disclosure of the right to request reasons within 60 days.
  - Saying the decision rested on "internal standards or policies", or on failing to reach a qualifying score, is insufficient.
  - Small creditors (≤150 applications in the preceding year) may notify orally.
  — [12 CFR 1002.9](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1002/section-1002.9)
- [L-US] The CFPB withdrew Circular 2023-03 (adverse-action reasons and the sample forms) effective 12 May 2025, as part of a 67-document withdrawal. The Reg B text itself is unchanged. — [CFPB withdrawn guidance](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/guidance/withdrawn-guidance/); [Federal Register 2025-08286](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2025/05/12/2025-08286/interpretive-rules-policy-statements-and-advisory-opinions-withdrawal)

### Inferences
- [R] **Lifecycle map for routing.**
  - *Application* → status, documents, channels.
  - *Assessment* → general factors only.
  - *Offer* → pre-contract form, APR / yıllık maliyet oranı, optional insurance.
  - *Disbursement* → withdrawal right.
  - *Repayment* → schedule, ara ödeme, gecikme.
  - *Closure* → payoff, "borcu yoktur", ipotek fek.
  Each stage has a different owner team: sales/underwriting, loan operations, collections, mortgage operations.
- **Playbook A — Application status ("başvurum ne durumda?", "kredim hâlâ onaylanmadı").**
  - *Determine:* applicant identity (authenticate first), product, application ID, current stage, and whether documents are missing.
  - *Ask:* "Which channel did you apply through, and when?"
  - *Check:* the status in the system, and any pending document or guarantor request.
  - *Options:* tell them the stage, list what is missing and how to upload it, give the expected next contact. Use only bank-published service levels; otherwise say "I can't give a time; you'll be notified via {kanal}".
  - *Never promise:* approval, amount, rate or date of decision.
  - *Escalate:* to underwriting/credit operations if the application is stuck past the published service level, or if the customer alleges they never applied (route to fraud, team 10).
- **Playbook B — Eligibility and documents.** Give the general criteria (income, existing debt, payment history, score) and the bank's published document list (Garanti example above). No personalised "you will qualify if…" statements [R].
- **Playbook C — Rejection ("kredim neden reddedildi?").**
  - *Determine:* whether a decision code or reason is available to front line. Many banks do not expose it [V].
  - *Options:* give general factors; say where to get the Findeks report; explain how to request a review or more information (in the EU from Nov 2026 a legal right to human review and explanation; in Türkiye the KVKK art. 11 objection right where the decision was solely automated); note that no fee may be charged for a rejected application (TCMB) [L-TR]. Suggest reapplying when circumstances change. Do not suggest "apply to many banks".
  - *Never:* give personalised financial advice; promise approval on reapplication; disclose internal cut-offs.
  - *Escalate:* to credit operations or the complaints team if discrimination, data error or identity issues are alleged.
- **Wording, rejection (TR):** "Başvurunuz bu aşamada olumlu sonuçlanmadı. Kredi kararları gelir, mevcut borçluluk, ödeme geçmişi ve kredi notu gibi kriterlerin birlikte değerlendirilmesiyle veriliyor; kararı ben değiştiremiyorum. İsterseniz değerlendirme hakkında {kanal} üzerinden bilgi talep edebilir, Findeks Risk Raporunuzu kontrol edebilir ve bilgileriniz değiştiğinde yeniden başvurabilirsiniz."
- **Wording, rejection (EN):** "Your application wasn't approved at this stage. Decisions weigh income, existing commitments, repayment history and credit score together, and I'm not able to change the outcome. You can request more information about the assessment via {channel}, check your credit report, and reapply if your circumstances change."

### Gaps
- No Turkish rule found that obliges lenders to give specific rejection reasons. Whether banks' front-line systems expose reason codes is institution-specific.
- Turnaround service levels for applications are not regulated in the sources reviewed; they are bank policy.

## 2. Türkiye lending specifics in 2026: maturity limits, LTV, taxes, credit-linked insurance, early repayment, partial prepayment, closure, mortgage release, restructuring

### Takeaway
BDDK sets maturity and LTV limits, and changes them by Board decision, sometimes several times a year (PERIODIC). Consumers have a statutory right to prepay with a full interest discount. Fixed-rate housing loans may carry a capped early-repayment compensation; consumer loans may carry no early-repayment fee. Credit-linked insurance is optional, can be bought from any insurer, and its unused premium is refundable on early closure. Two BDDK restructuring windows (July 2025 and January 2026, 48 months) have both closed. As of Oct 2026 restructuring is again bank-discretionary within the normal maturity limits [V].

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] General consumer loan maturity limit (BDDK 11152, 13.02.2025): ≤125,000 TL → 36 months; 125,000–250,000 TL → 24 months; >250,000 TL → 12 months. **The same limits apply to restructurings.** — [BDDK 11152](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1270). [B: Garanti BBVA] applies this to loans opened after 13 Feb 2025, based on remaining principal. The same Garanti page also says "60 aya varan vadeler", an internal inconsistency explained by exempt loan types. — [Garanti BBVA](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/krediler)
- [L-TR] Categories exempt from the general consumer maturity limit (Kredi Yön. art. 12(3)): home renovation that becomes an integral part of the home, other real-estate purchases, education fees, payments of public debts made directly to the authority, and restructurings of these. — [Bankaların Kredi İşlemlerine İlişkin Yönetmelik](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=40520&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] Consumer loans for **mobile phones** (BDDK 11582, 01.10.2026): price ≤40,000 TL → 12 months; >40,000 TL → 3 months. Refurbished phones sold by an authorised refurbisher or dealer: ≤50,000 TL → 12 months; >50,000 TL → 3 months. — [BDDK 11582](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1348)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **Vehicle loans, general** (BDDK 10099, 21.02.2022, the latest general decision on BDDK's list) [V: confirm still in force]:

  | Final invoice value | Max maturity | Max loan-to-value |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≤400k TL | 48 months | 70% |
  | 400k–800k TL | 36 months | 50% |
  | 800k–1.2M TL | 24 months | 30% |
  | 1.2M–2M TL | 12 months | 20% |
  | >2M TL | — | 0% (no loan) |

  — [BDDK 10099](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1099). For second-hand cars, value = kasko (insurance) value (Kredi Yön. art. 12(2)). — [Kredi Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=40520&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **Domestic-producer electric vehicles only** (KVK Geçici 12 producers; BDDK 11158, 06.03.2025):

  | Value | Max maturity | Max loan-to-value |
  |---|---|---|
  | ≤2.5M TL | 48 months | 70% |
  | 2.5–5M TL | 36 months | 50% |
  | 5–6.5M TL | 24 months | 30% |
  | 6.5–7.5M TL | 12 months | 20% |
  | >7.5M TL | — | 0% |

  Restructurings keep the original maturity. — [BDDK 11158](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1278)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **Mortgage loan-to-value** (BDDK 11364, 29.01.2026). This applies to home purchases and to home-secured loans other than vehicle loans. The first-hand/second-hand distinction is abolished. Rows are home value; columns are energy class.

  | Home value | A–B | C | Other |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | ≤5M TL | 90% | 80% | 70% |
  | 5–7M TL | 80% | 70% | 60% |
  | 7–10M TL | 70% | 60% | 50% |
  | 10–20M TL | 50% | 40% | 30% |
  | >20M TL | 40% | 30% | 20% |

  — [BDDK 11364](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1327); [BDDK press release 30.01.2026](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Duyuru/EkGetir/2157?ekId=889)
- [L-TR] Where the consumer, spouse or minor children already own ≥1 home, the LTV ratios are **reduced by 75%**. Village homes are excluded from the test, as are deed shares ≤50% (unless the person holds shares in several homes). This rule from decision 10656 (24.08.2023) continues under 11364. — [BDDK 10656](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1191). Collateral must be valued by authorised valuation firms (Kredi Yön. art. 12(1)). — [Kredi Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=40520&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **Taxes:**
  - KKDF is 15% on consumer-loan interest. — [GİB özelge (search extract)](https://gib.gov.tr/mevzuat/kanun/445/ozelge/36719)
  - BSMV on consumer loans was raised from 10% to 15% by Presidential Decision 7345. — [TÜRMOB e-kütüphane, July 2023](https://www.turmob.org.tr/ekutuphane/Read/7fea8441-b31e-46fa-91e7-cf9f96b4248c)
  - Comparison sites state 15% KKDF + 15% BSMV on interest, with housing loans exempt. — [Hangikredi](https://www.hangikredi.com/bilgi-merkezi/kredi-hesaplamasi-nasil-yapilir/), [HesapKurdu](https://www.hesapkurdu.com/ihtiyac-kredisi/rehber/bsmv-nedir-nasil-hesaplanir)
  - **Conflict:** hesap.com still states 5% BSMV and is likely outdated. — [hesap.com](https://hesap.com/kredi/kredi-hesaplama). The Presidential decision is the more authoritative source [V: confirm current rates against GİB].
- [L-TR] **Credit-linked insurance** (6502 arts. 29, 38, amended 2022):
  - It cannot be arranged without the consumer's explicit written or durable request.
  - The lender must also offer a version of the contract without insurance.
  - It must accept cover from **any insurer the consumer chooses**.
  - The cover must match the remaining debt and term.
  - The loan cannot be tied to unrelated products.
  — [6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf)
- [L-TR] TKS Yön. art. 26 (2022 amendment):
  - On early repayment or restructuring, credit-linked "meblağ" (fixed-sum, e.g., life) insurance **ends**, unless the consumer gives informed explicit consent to continue or adjust it.
  - A compatible policy from another insurer naming the lender as loss-payee (dain-i mürtehin) must be accepted without changing the loan terms.
  - No duplicate cover, and no cover above the loan amount.
  — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [L-TR] The Ministry of Trade guidance says that on early closure the consumer can end the policy with a petition and reclaim the premium for the unused days (gün esaslı iade). On restructuring, cover and term can be re-set. Withdrawing from the loan within 14 days also ends linked services. — [Ticaret Bakanlığı – Tüketici Kredisi Bilgilendirme](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/yayinlar/tuketici-bilgi-rehberi/tuketici-kredisi-sozlesmeleri-hakkinda-bilgilendirme)
- [L-TR] SEDDK guidance on BKBS (individual-credit-linked) life insurance:
  - If the customer presents a compatible policy within one month of the policy start, the bank's policy is cancelled from inception and premiums are refunded within 5 business days with no deduction.
  - The withdrawal period is 15 days from notification, or one month after the first premium if the customer was not informed.
  - Policies longer than one year can be cancelled after one year without the lender's consent.
  — [SEDDK](https://www.seddk.gov.tr/tr/mevzuat-bkbs-hayat-sigortasi)
- [L-TR] **Early repayment (erken kapama), consumer loans:**
  - The consumer may prepay one or more installments, or the whole loan, and the lender must discount **all** interest and cost elements (6502 art. 27).
  - The discount is computed as of the **actual payment date**, which cannot be changed to the consumer's detriment (TKS Yön. art. 15).
  - **Payoff on an installment date** = the due installment + the remaining principal + any uncollected interest and taxes.
  - **Payoff between installments** = interest accrued on principal since the last paid installment date + the remaining principal + taxes (art. 16).
  - [L-TR, inference] Because TCMB allows only the allocation fee on consumer loans, no separate early-repayment fee is permitted.
  — [6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf); [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf); [Tebliğ 2020/7](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR] **Housing finance (konut finansmanı):**
  - The same discount duty applies (art. 37(1)).
  - **Fixed-rate loans:** if the contract provides for it, early-repayment compensation (erken ödeme tazminatı) is capped at **1%** of the prepaid amount where the remaining term is ≤36 months and **2%** where it is >36 months.
  - **Variable-rate loans:** no compensation.
  - TCMB adds that no other fee may be charged and the compensation cannot exceed the interest discount granted.
  — [6502 art. 37](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf); [Tebliğ 2020/7 art. 10(4)](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5); confirmed by [B: Garanti BBVA Mortgage SSS](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/mortgage)
- [L-TR] **Partial prepayment (ara ödeme)** (TKS Yön. art. 17):
  - Any prepaid amount of at least one installment counts as ara ödeme.
  - Interest since the previous installment date plus taxes is collected first; the rest reduces principal.
  - **Default:** the number and dates of installments stay the same and the installment amount drops.
  - **On request:** a payment holiday for the number of installments prepaid; or, by written or durable agreement, a different number of installments or different dates.
  - The new plan is provided **free**, in writing or on a durable medium. The burden of proof is on the lender.
  — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [L-TR] **Acceleration (muacceliyet)** for fixed-term consumer loans requires **≥2 consecutive installments in default** and a **≥30-day warning**. Accelerated installments exclude future interest and commission (6502 art. 28; TKS Yön. art. 18(1)). Housing finance has the same rule (art. 34). — [6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf)
- [L-TR] **Default interest** is capped at the contractual rate + 30%. It is computed on the **principal portion** of the late amount × days late, plus taxes, using the actual payment date. Over-collection must be refunded with interest (TKS Yön. art. 18(2)–(3)). **No compound interest**, including in default (6502 art. 4(7); TKS Yön. art. 22(4)). — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [L-TR] **Closure:**
  - An account opened only for a fixed-term loan carries no fees and closes automatically when the loan is repaid, unless the customer asks otherwise (6502 art. 31; Tebliğ art. 10(7)).
  - The "no-debt letter" (borcu yoktur / borcu bulunmadığına dair yazı) may not be charged for once (bir defaya mahsus).
  - Copies of contracts and receipts are free in the first year (Tebliğ arts. 9(4), 10(5)).
  — [Tebliğ 2020/7](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR] **Mortgage release (ipotek fekki):** only amounts paid to third parties may be charged for establishing, changing or releasing a mortgage (TCMB fee classification, item 1.3). Land-registry encumbrance-search costs may be passed on (Talimat 7(i)). — [TCMB Uygulama Talimatı, 13 Jan 2026](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751/2023-39+say%C4%B1l%C4%B1+FT+Tebli%C4%9Fi+Uygulama+Talimat%C4%B1+-13+Ocak+2026.pdf?MOD=AJPERES&CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE-9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751-pKSwC.I)
- [I-TR] Secondary source: the mortgage does not lift automatically. After closure the bank sends an electronic release letter (e-fek) to the land registry via TAKBİS, and the registry then removes the mortgage. — [Hangikredi](https://www.hangikredi.com/bilgi-merkezi/konut-kredisinde-tapu-ve-ipotek-ile-ilgili-merak-edilenler/) [V: check timing with the bank's process]
- [L-TR] **BDDK restructuring windows:**
  - **11240 (10.07.2025):** cards with an unpaid statement balance, or already restructured; consumer loans (including KMH) that were delayed or restructured. Request within 3 months; up to 48 months.
  - **11366 (29.01.2026):** cards with a statement balance partly or wholly unpaid at the due date; consumer loans (including KMH) more than 30 days past due as of the decision date. Request within 3 months; up to 48 months, with no new lending.
  - Card conditions in both: the restructuring installment is added to the monthly minimum; no limit increase until 50% is repaid; any amount above the limit is not treated as over-limit.
  - The wording is "yapılandırılabilmesine imkân" ("may be restructured"), i.e., permissive, not an obligation on banks.
  — [BDDK 11240](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1301); [BDDK 11366](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1325)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] TCMB: the monthly contractual rate on restructured card debt may not exceed the **reference rate (3.11%)**. — [TCMB max rates page](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Istatistikler/Bankacilik+Verileri/Kredi_Karti_Islemlerinde_Uygulanacak_Azami_Faiz_Oranlari). A secondary source dates the end of the January 2026 window as 29 April 2026. — [HesapKurdu](https://www.hesapkurdu.com/kredi-karti/rehber/bddk-kredi-ve-kredi-karti-kararlari)
- [L-TR] **Loan classification:**
  - More than 30 days and up to 90 days past due → Group 2 (yakın izleme, close monitoring).
  - More than 90 days and up to 180 days → Group 3 (non-performing, "donuk alacak").
  - Restructured loans are monitored, and a further delay of more than 30 days within the one-year monitoring period moves them to non-performing.
  — [Kredilerin Sınıflandırılması Yönetmeliği](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=22599&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)

### Inferences
- **Playbook D — Schedule, installment amount and date questions ("taksitim neden değişti?", "ödeme planımı görebilir miyim?").** Check whether there was an ara ödeme (installment reduced by default) or a restructuring. A fixed-rate consumer loan's rate cannot have risen [L-TR]. Re-send the current plan; the first year is free [L-TR]. Escalate to loan operations if the system plan conflicts with the contract.
- **Playbook E — Early repayment and payoff ("kredimi erken kapatmak istiyorum", "erken kapama tutarı ne kadar?").**
  - *Determine:* loan type (consumer, housing, vehicle), fixed or variable rate, the intended payment date, and whether the customer wants full or partial prepayment.
  - *Check:* the system-generated payoff amount for **that date**, any housing-loan compensation (fixed rate only, 1% or 2%), and linked insurance.
  - *Explain:* the payoff equals the remaining principal plus interest accrued since the last installment plus taxes. It changes daily, so only a quote for the stated date is valid.
  - *Options:* for partial prepayment, ask whether they prefer a lower installment (the default) or a payment holiday / different term, which needs a written request [L-TR]. Mention the insurance refund route, via petition or the bank's process, at pro-rata (day-based) value.
  - *Never:* compute a payoff by hand from approximate data; promise an insurance refund amount; promise the KKB record "disappears".
  - *Escalate:* to loan operations for disputed discount calculations, and to the bancassurance team or insurer for premium refunds.
  - *Wording (TR):* "Erken kapama tutarı ödemeyi yapacağınız güne göre hesaplanır: kalan anapara, son taksit tarihinden ödeme gününe kadar işleyen faiz ve bunun vergilerinden oluşur. Sistemde {tarih} için görünen kapama tutarı {tutar} TL; başka bir gün ödemeniz halinde tutar değişir. Krediye bağlı hayat sigortanız varsa, kullanılmayan güne ait prim iadesi için {süreç} işletilir."
- **Playbook F — Closure documents and mortgage release ("borcu yoktur yazısı istiyorum", "ipoteğim hâlâ kalkmadı").**
  - Issue or route the no-debt letter; the first one is free [L-TR].
  - For a mortgage: confirm closure, check that the e-fek was sent, and tell the customer which third-party costs may apply (land registry) [L-TR].
  - *Escalate:* to mortgage operations if the release is not visible after the bank's standard period [V].
  - *Never:* tell the customer to visit the land registry before checking whether the bank sent the release.
- **Playbook G — Insurance ("sigorta zorunlu mu?", "kredimi kapattım sigorta parası iade olur mu?").**
  - Credit-linked insurance is optional and can come from any insurer [L-TR].
  - Bringing a compatible policy within 1 month cancels the bank's policy with a full refund (SEDDK) [L-TR].
  - On early closure, the cover ends and the unused premium is refundable pro rata, unless the customer explicitly chose to keep the cover [L-TR].
  - Separate DASK (mandatory earthquake insurance) from optional life cover [V: DASK detail not researched here].
- [R] The validity of any figure quoted from these BDDK tables depends on the decision date. The agent must not quote maturity or LTV limits from memory; read them from a maintained rules table that carries the BDDK decision number and date.

## 3. Türkiye credit-card financials in 2026: minimum payment, interest, taxes, cash advance, restructuring, consequences, statements

### Takeaway
As of Oct 2026, BDDK 11581 (01.10.2026) sets the minimum payment at **20% of the statement balance for cards with a limit ≤100,000 TL and 40% above that**. TCMB caps monthly rates in tiers by statement balance; the 1/10/2026 row is unchanged since 1/1/2026. Interest applies only to the unpaid part. If the customer pays at least the minimum, they pay contractual interest (akdi) on the rest. If they pay less, late interest (gecikme) applies on the unpaid part of the minimum and contractual interest on the rest. No compound interest is allowed. Repeated non-payment closes the card: 3 misses in a calendar year close it to cash; 3 consecutive misses close it to everything; non-payment within 3 months, plus 1 month after notice, cancels it.

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR] Definitions (5464 art. 3):
  - **Son ödeme tarihi** (payment due date): the last day to pay the statement balance or the minimum without being late.
  - **Dönem borcu** (statement balance): debits and credits up to the statement date plus the previous statement balance.
  - **Asgari tutar** (minimum payment): the least amount of the statement balance that must be paid.
  — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **Minimum payment** (BDDK 11581, 01.10.2026): limit ≤100,000 TL → 20% of the statement balance; limit >100,000 TL → 40%. — [BDDK 11581](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1349). The previous rule was a 50,000 TL threshold (10970, 26.09.2024). — [BDDK 10970](https://www.bddk.org.tr/Mevzuat/DokumanGetir/1255)
- [L-TR] By law the contractual minimum cannot be below 20% of the statement balance. The Board may raise it to 40% and differentiate it by cardholder group (5464 art. 24; Yön. art. 22(7)). — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf). **Discrepancy:** the Findeks FAQ says banks set the ratio "not exceeding" the BDDK rate, which is inconsistent with the statutory floor; the law prevails. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular). [B: VakıfBank] already shows the 100k/20%/40% rule. — [Vakıfkart Ekstre Açıklamaları](https://www.vakifkart.com.tr/ayricaliklar/destek/ekstre-aciklamalari)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] **TCMB maximum monthly rates, valid 1/10/2026:**
  - Reference rate: 3.11%.
  - TL purchases, statement balance <30,000 TL: **3.25%** contractual / **3.55%** late.
  - 30,000–180,000 TL: **3.75% / 4.05%**.
  - >180,000 TL (and all corporate cards): **4.25% / 4.55%**.
  - **Cash withdrawals and cash-like transactions, and KMH:** 4.25% / 4.55%.
  - Foreign-currency (FX) balances: 2.98% / 3.28%.
  - Rates are announced on the fifth-to-last business day of each month; banks choose freely below the cap.
  — [TCMB](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Istatistikler/Bankacilik+Verileri/Kredi_Karti_Islemlerinde_Uygulanacak_Azami_Faiz_Oranlari). The tier thresholds were raised from 1 Jan 2026. — [TCMB 2025 annual report §2.3](https://www3.tcmb.gov.tr/yillikrapor/2025/m-2-3/). **Stale bank page:** VakıfBank still shows the Nov-2024 tiers (25k/150k TL; 3.50/4.25/4.75%). — [Vakıfkart](https://www.vakifkart.com.tr/ayricaliklar/destek/ekstre-aciklamalari)
- [L-TR] **Interest mechanics** (5464 art. 26; Card Yön. art. 20):
  - No interest may run from a date before the statement date, **except on cash use, which can accrue from the transaction date**.
  - "Cash-like" transactions include precious metals, foreign currency, balance transfers to pay another card, payments to persons or institutions, gambling, securities, travellers' cheques and money transfers.
  - On partial payment, interest runs on the **remaining balance**.
  - **If at least the minimum is paid → contractual interest (akdi) on the remainder. If less than the minimum is paid → late interest (gecikme) on the unpaid part of the minimum, and contractual interest on the remainder above the minimum.**
  - No compound interest.
  - There must be at least 10 days between the statement date and the due date.
  — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf); [Kart Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=11180&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
  - **Conflict:** the Findeks FAQ says that if the minimum is not paid, late interest applies "to the whole statement balance". This contradicts Yön. art. 20(3) and the banks' worked examples. Follow the regulation. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [B: Yapı Kredi] Worked method: interest = balance × monthly rate × days ÷ 30.
  - Statement date → due date: contractual interest on the amount left unpaid at the due date.
  - Due date → next statement date: late interest on the unpaid part of the minimum, contractual interest on the rest.
  - Example (illustrative rates 3.50%/3.80%, 40% minimum, 1,000 TL spend, 100 TL paid, 10 days then 20 days): 900 × 3.50% × 10/30 = 10.50; 300 × 3.80% × 20/30 = 7.60; 600 × 3.50% × 20/30 = 14.00; total 32.10 TL before taxes.
  - The page mislabels the last step as 3.80%, which shows why agents must recompute rather than copy.
  — [Yapı Kredi](https://www.yapikredi.com.tr/bireysel-bankacilik/kartlar/akdi-ve-gecikme-faizi-hesaplama-ornekleri)
- [B: VakıfBank] Taxes and payments:
  - BSMV is applied to cash-advance fees, card fees and interest; KKDF to interest accrued on the statement date. Displayed rates exclude KKDF and BSMV.
  - Monthly rates are annualised simply (e.g., "%3,50 (yıllık %42)").
  - Payments are allocated in this order: prior late interest and its charges → contractual interest and its charges → carried-over debt → cash used in the period → new spending.
  - Unpaid FX balances are converted at the TCMB selling rate + 1%.
  — [Vakıfkart](https://www.vakifkart.com.tr/ayricaliklar/destek/ekstre-aciklamalari)
- [L-TR] **Cash advance fees:** no fee on KMH credit or installment cash advances. The cash-advance fee is capped at **1% of the advance** in the Tebliğ text (art. 10(6)). TCMB raises "maximum fees" annually by CPI, rounded to 2 decimals (Talimat 4(a)). — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5); [Talimat](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751/2023-39+say%C4%B1l%C4%B1+FT+Tebli%C4%9Fi+Uygulama+Talimat%C4%B1+-13+Ocak+2026.pdf?MOD=AJPERES&CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE-9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751-pKSwC.I). **Unreconciled:** [B: VakıfBank] lists a 3.87% + 0.55 TL (excluding BSMV) domestic cash-withdrawal fee. — [Vakıfkart](https://www.vakifkart.com.tr/ayricaliklar/destek/ekstre-aciklamalari) [V: check TCMB's current indexed maximum].
- [L-TR] **No fee** for deferring or installmentizing the statement balance, adding installments, extending the due date, or installment cash advances. Statements, virtual cards and up to 2 card renewals a year are also free (Talimat 8(j),(l)). - [L-TR] **Consequences of non-payment:**
  - Minimum unpaid **3 times in total in a calendar year** → card closed to cash use.
  - **3 times consecutively** → closed to cash and purchases.
  - Limits frozen until the whole statement balance is paid (Yön. art. 22(6)).
  - Minimum unpaid within **3 months** after the due date and not paid within **1 month** of the issuer's notice → **all of that issuer's cards are cancelled**, with no new card until the debt is paid in full (5464 art. 8).
  — [Kart Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=11180&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5); [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf)
- [B: Ziraat Bankası] Stricter policy: after **2** consecutive missed or partial minimum payments, the card is closed to cash and purchases, and reopens when the minimum is paid. — [Ziraat SSS](https://www.ziraatbank.com.tr/tr/sss/kart-ve-uye-isyeri/kartlar)
- [L-TR] **Contract changes** are notified on the statement and apply from that period's due date. Interest increases need **30 days'** prior notice. A cardholder who pays everything within **60 days** and stops using the card is not affected. Requests to reduce the limit, cancel the card or terminate the contract must be completed within **7 days** (Yön. art. 18). — [Kart Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=11180&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5). A cancellation cannot be refused because a balance is outstanding, and no annual fee is due while the balance is repaid (Tebliğ art. 11(2)). — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)

- **Playbook L1 — "Asgariyi ödedim, neden faiz geldi?" ("I paid the minimum, why was I charged interest?")**

  - *Show:* the formula with system values only.

Ancak ödenmeyen {kalan} TL kredilendirilmiş sayıldığı için hesap kesim tarihinden itibaren akdi faiz işledi: {kalan} × %{oran} × {gün}/30 = {faiz} TL; buna KKDF ve BSMV olarak {vergi} TL eklendi. ## 4. KMH interest, Turkish fee rules, and a 5-step framework for explaining an unexpected fee

### Takeaway
KMH (overdraft) interest accrues daily on the amount used, capped at the TCMB cash or KMH maximum (4.25% contractual / 4.55% late a month, PERIODIC). Turkish fee law is a closed-list system: a fee is lawful only if it is in TCMB's classification and in the contract or tariff, with consent. Fee increases need 30 days' notice and, if above CPI, separate consent. Explaining an unexpected fee is therefore an evidence check, not a defence of the charge.

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] The TCMB maximum rate for cash and cash-like use also applies to KMH ("Kredili mevduat hesapları için de geçerlidir"): 4.25% contractual / 4.55% late from 1/10/2026. — [TCMB](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/TR/TCMB+TR/Main+Menu/Istatistikler/Bankacilik+Verileri/Kredi_Karti_Islemlerinde_Uygulanacak_Azami_Faiz_Oranlari)
- [I-TR] KMH interest accrues **daily** on the amount used. For example, 1,000 TL repaid after 10 days accrues 10 days of interest. Loan interest, by contrast, runs on the remaining principal per installment period. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [L-TR] No fee may be charged on KMH credit (Tebliğ art. 10(6)). For overdrafts above the agreed limit lasting more than a month, the lender must inform the customer of the excess, amount, rate and late rate (TKS Yön. art. 21). — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5); [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [L-TR] **Fee system** (Tebliğ 2020/7, RG 07.03.2020 no. 31061, as amended):
  - Consent is required for every fee-bearing product. A fee for a service not priced in the contract requires prior information and consent, and the institution bears the proof (art. 5(3)–(4)).
  - Only items in the Annex-1 classification may be charged. New fee items require TCMB permission. Third-party costs may be passed on at cost (art. 6).
  - Fee **increases** need ≥**30 days'** notice in writing, on a durable medium or by recorded phone. The customer may stop using the service within **15 days** without paying the increase. Continued use counts as acceptance (art. 7).
  - Increases above CPI require **separate consent**. Increases are not retroactive. Campaign discounts that end on a pre-announced date need no notice (Talimat 4(a),(d),(e)).
  - Refunds decided by the bank or an authority go free of charge to the customer's account, with notification (art. 8; Talimat 5).
  - Fees must be published under "Ürün ve Hizmet Ücretleri"; banks must provide calculators; no fees for notifications made without consent (art. 9).
  — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5); [TCMB Talimat, 13 Jan 2026](https://www.tcmb.gov.tr/wps/wcm/connect/9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751/2023-39+say%C4%B1l%C4%B1+FT+Tebli%C4%9Fi+Uygulama+Talimat%C4%B1+-13+Ocak+2026.pdf?MOD=AJPERES&CACHEID=ROOTWORKSPACE-9fcd654e-65a7-4c42-8292-06ec3846e751-pKSwC.I)
- [L-TR] **Annual card fees** (art. 11):
  - Issuers must offer at least one card with no annual fee, assessed by the same criteria. A rejected no-fee application cannot be converted into a fee-paying card.
  - On request, a fee-paying card must be switched to a no-fee card with the same limit.
  - No fee on debit or virtual cards. Supplementary cards cost at most 50% of the main card fee.
  - The fee accrues only after the first debt-creating use.
  - Cards with no activity for ≥180 days carry no fee while inactive. Such fees may be held for up to 12 months and are then cancelled; they bear no interest, no enforcement and no KKB or Risk Merkezi reporting.
  — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR, PERIODIC] Consumer disputes valued **below 186,000 TL** must go to Tüketici Hakem Heyeti (consumer arbitration committees) from 1 Jan 2026 (Tebliğ RG 23.12.2025 no. 33116; revaluation 25.49%). — [Ticaret Bakanlığı](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/tuketici-hakem-heyetlerine-basvurularda-2026-yili-parasal-degerleri-yeniden-belirlendi); [Afyon Ticaret İl Md.](https://afyon.ticaret.gov.tr/duyurular/tuketici-hakem-heyeti-basvurularinda-2026-yili-parasal-degerleri-yeniden-belirlendi)

### Inferences
- **Five-step framework for an unexpected fee [R, grounded in the TR law above]:**
  1. **Identify the line item.** Read the exact statement or account entry: date, description, amount, any BSMV line. *Ask:* "Which line, which date?"
  2. **Map it to a fee type and the tariff.** Find the Annex-1 category and the bank's published tariff ("Ürün ve Hizmet Ücretleri"), and check that the product contract includes it. If the fee is not in the classification or the contract, flag it as potentially unlawful and escalate.
  3. **Check notification and consent where relevant.** For an increase, look for 30 days' notice and, if above CPI, separate consent. For a one-off service, look for prior consent. Check for statutory exemptions: KMH, installment cash advance, statement deferral, the first no-debt letter, the first-year document copy, inactive-card annual fee, loan-account fees.
  4. **Explain the calculation only from verified data,** e.g., "{oran}% × {tutar} + BSMV {vergi}". If the basis is not visible, say so and open a check. Never invent rates or amounts.
  5. **Options.** A refund request per policy (refunds go free to the account) [L-TR]; a fee-free alternative (no-fee card switch, e-statement); a complaint reference number; the external route (Tüketici Hakem Heyeti below 186,000 TL in 2026).
  - *Escalate:* fee not in the tariff, missing consent or notice evidence, many affected customers (possible systemic error) → complaints team (04) or product/back office.
- *Wording (TR):* "Ekstrenizde {tarih} tarihli {açıklama} satırındaki {tutar} TL'nin {ücret türü} olduğunu görüyorum. Bu ücret, sözleşmenizde ve bankamızın yayımladığı ücret tarifesinde {oran/tutar} olarak yer alıyor; {bildirim tarihi} tarihinde size bildirilmiş. Hesaplaması: {formül}. İsterseniz itiraz/iade talebinizi kayda alabilirim; sonuç {kanal} ile bildirilir."
- *Wording when the data are uncertain (TR):* "Bu ücretin dayanağını sistemde net olarak göremiyorum; yanlış bilgi vermemek için tahminde bulunmayacağım. Talebinizi {referans} numarasıyla inceleme ekibine iletiyorum, sonuç {süre} içinde {kanal} ile bildirilecek."

## 5. Explanation templates for interest and charges: APR vs nominal rate, daily accrual, grace periods, minimum-payment cost, amortization, presenting uncertainty

### Takeaway
Turkish card and KMH rates are quoted **monthly**, and annual figures are simple multiples (×12) because compound interest is prohibited. Loan contracts must show the effective annual rate. Templates must work from placeholders filled only with system data, show the formula, and state what is known, what is general rule, and what is unknown.

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR] The effective annual interest rate (EYFO) is mandatory in consumer loan contracts and excludes breach fees (TKS Yön. art. 14). — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf). [B: Garanti BBVA] says its displayed "yıllık maliyet oranı" excludes the insurance premium. — [Garanti](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/krediler)
- [L-TR] No compound interest in consumer transactions, including default (6502 art. 4(7)); card monthly rates are annualised ×12 in bank disclosures (e.g., 3.50% = 42% a year). — [6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf); [Vakıfkart](https://www.vakifkart.com.tr/ayricaliklar/destek/ekstre-aciklamalari)
- [L-TR] **Grace period (card):** there are at least 10 days between the statement and the due date. Interest runs only on amounts left unpaid, and not from before the statement date, so paying the full statement balance by the due date means no purchase interest. Cash use can accrue from the transaction date (5464 art. 26). — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf)
- [I-TR] Paying only the minimum "fulfils the legal obligation" but makes the debt grow, because the remainder keeps accruing interest. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [L-US] On card statements, Reg Z requires a **"Minimum Payment Warning"**, the payoff time and total cost if only minimums are paid, and the monthly payment and savings for repayment in 36 months (§1026.7(b)(12)). — [12 CFR 1026.7](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-12/chapter-X/part-1026/subpart-B/section-1026.7)
- [L-TR] The **loan payoff composition** is set out in TKS Yön. art. 16, and ara ödeme rules (lower installment by default) in art. 17. — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)

## 6. Credit reporting: KKB/Findeks in Türkiye; UK CRAs and US FCRA disputes (brief)

### Takeaway
In Türkiye, banks report to the TBB Risk Merkezi and KKB, and Findeks shows the consumer's report and score. **KKB cannot correct data.** Corrections go through the reporting bank, via the Findeks objection form or the KVKK right to correction. For accounts closed after 26 Mar 2024, negative history is shown for **10 years** from the latest relevant date; open VYŞ-held debts are shown until paid. The agent must never promise removal of accurate records.

### Cited Findings
- [I-TR] **The Findeks report shows:** all active accounts and accounts closed within 10 years (loans, cards, KMH), with limits, balances, payment performance, delinquency details, and any legal follow-up or VYŞ transfer. Data come from banks and finance companies, and new data can take **up to 2 days** to appear. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [I-TR] **Retention:**
  - Accounts closed **after 26 Mar 2024**: shown for **10 years** from the latest of closure, legal follow-up, collection or VYŞ-transfer date.
  - Accounts under legal follow-up and then paid: 10 years from the closing date.
  - VYŞ accounts: open ones shown **indefinitely until paid**; closed ones for 10 years after closure.
  - Older accounts are subject to the earlier 5-year transitional rule.
  — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [I-TR] **Score not computed** when, among other cases: debt is >90 days past due and unpaid; there was a VYŞ transfer within the last 12 months; an open VYŞ debt exists; or the person has no credit product older than 3 months. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [I-TR] **Objections:** use the Findeks "Risk Raporu İtiraz" form after 2+ days. It is forwarded to the bank and Findeks says it is resolved within **24 hours**, with the result emailed. **KKB has no right or discretion to delete or correct records.** — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular)
- [L-TR] KVKK rights to correction (art. 11(1)(d)) and to a reply within 30 days (art. 13). — [KVKK 6698](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6698.pdf)
- [L-TR] **2022 "sicil affı"** (5834 Geçici md. 4): records for debts with due dates before 1/10/2022 are **disregarded, not deleted**, if the overdue part was paid or restructured by 1/7/2023. — [Findeks SSS](https://www.findeks.com/sikca-sorulan-sorular). [B: Boğaziçi VYŞ] describes the amnesty as "6 ay içinde" ("within 6 months"), which looks outdated or imprecise. — [Boğaziçi VYŞ SSS](https://bogazicivarlik.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/)
- [L-TR] **KVKK case:** a person wrongly pursued because their ID number was mistakenly attached to someone else's NPL file still appeared in the Risk Merkezi after notice. The VYŞ was fined for continuing calls and failing to correct UYAP and Risk Merkezi records (KVKK 2021/424). — [KVKK 2021/424](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7114/2021-424)
- [L-UK] **UK credit reference agencies:**
  - There are three main CRAs: Equifax, Experian and TransUnion.
  - A **statutory report is free**.
  - Disputes go to the CRA and/or the original lender. The lender is responsible for its entries, and CRAs cannot amend them without the lender.
  - A **Notice of Correction** can be added.
  - Accurate defaults may remain even after hardship forbearance. Where the creditor stops pursuing the debt, showing money still owed "would appear unfair".
  - The ICO guidance is under review after the **Data (Use and Access) Act**.
  — [ICO – Credit](https://ico.org.uk/for-the-public/credit/)
- [L-US] **FCRA:**
  - Consumer reporting agencies must reinvestigate disputes **free within 30 days**, extendable by 15 days if the consumer adds information. A "frivolous" determination must be notified within 5 business days (15 U.S.C. §1681i).
  - Most adverse items, including collections and charge-offs, become obsolete after **7 years**; bankruptcies after **10 years** (§1681c).
  — [§1681i](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681i); [§1681c](https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/1681c)

### Inferences
- **Playbook K — "Kredi notum düştü / Findeks'te gecikme görünüyor, sildirin" ("my score dropped / remove the delinquency").**
  - *Determine:* whether the entry is accurate (a payment really was late or missed) or disputed (wrong person, wrong amount or date, paid but shown as unpaid).
  - *Check:* payment history, the reporting date, and whether 2+ days have passed since the payment.
  - *Options:* if the entry is accurate, explain retention and how future on-time payment helps. If it is inaccurate, open a correction with the reporting team and/or point to the Findeks objection form, and note the KVKK 30-day timeline.
  - *Never:* promise deletion, a score change or a timeline for the score.
  - *Escalate:* identity mismatch → fraud (10) and data protection; systemic reporting error → credit reporting operations.
  - *Wording (TR):* "Findeks'teki kayıtlar bankaların bildirimlerine dayanır; KKB kayıtları kendisi değiştiremez. Kayıt doğruysa silinmesi mümkün değil ve kapanıştan itibaren 10 yıl görüntülenebilir; hatalı olduğunu düşünüyorsanız düzeltme talebinizi hemen kayda alıyorum."

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on the exact day-past-due threshold at which Turkish banks report a "gecikme" status, or on reporting frequency to KKB [V].
- UK default retention (commonly six years) was not confirmed in the ICO page I reviewed [V].

## 7. Arrears and collections operations, inbound and outbound

### Takeaway
A compliant collections conversation follows this order:

| Step | What happens |
|---|---|
| 1 | Identify yourself and the purpose |
| 2 | Verify you are speaking to the account holder (right-party contact) **before** any debt detail |
| 3 | Disclose nothing to third parties, including on voicemail and to relatives |
| 4 | Understand the cause of the arrears and screen for vulnerability |
| 5 | Agree only affordable, sustainable arrangements, or capture a specific promise to pay (PTP) |
| 6 | Refer to free debt advice where relevant |
| 7 | Document everything |
| 8 | Hand off to humans for hardship decisions, disputes, legal stages and crises |

Turkey's hard rules here come mainly from:
- KVKK: fines for disclosing debts to relatives or third parties and for using unverified numbers.
- 6502 / TKS Yön.: 2 consecutive installments plus a 30-day notice before acceleration, and a late-interest cap.
- 5464: card cancellation timeline.
- Classification regulation: non-performing after 90 days.

I found no Turkish statutory numeric limits on call times or frequency [V]. The UK (CONC 7) and the US (Reg F) supply detailed conduct benchmarks.

### Cited Findings
**Türkiye — stages and legal steps**
- [L-TR] Early arrears (>30 days) = Group 2 close monitoring; >90 days = non-performing (Group 3) and onward. — [Sınıflandırma Yön.](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=22599&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR] Acceleration requires ≥2 consecutive missed installments plus a ≥30-day notice (6502 art. 28). Card: cancellation after 3 months of unpaid minimum plus 1 month after notice (5464 art. 8). — [6502](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.6502.pdf); [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf)
- [I-TR] Common practice (secondary sources): a formal notice (ihtarname) is sent around or before 90 days past due, followed by legal follow-up through contracted law firms and enforcement (icra) proceedings. — [hesap.com](https://hesap.com/hesap-defteri/kredi-karti-borcu-odenmezse-ne-olur); [HesapKurdu](https://www.hesapkurdu.com/ihtiyac-kredisi/rehber/kredi-borcu-yasal-takip-sureci) [V: each bank's timeline]
- [L-TR / B] A KVKK decision describes one bank's process: in-house teams call late customers first, then files are passed to external law firms for "idari ve yasal takip". The bank remains the **data controller**, and its rule is that **no information is given without customer verification**. — [KVKK 2021/115](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6928/2021-115)
- [B: Boğaziçi VYŞ] **NPL sale to VYŞ:**
  - Debts are transferred by assignment contract (alacak temlik).
  - The VYŞ contacts debtors and may offer discounts.
  - A signed payment plan (taahhüt belgesi) sets out installments, dates and channel. If installments are missed, the plan lapses; amounts already paid are deducted.
  - Releasing seizures (haciz fekki) at the enforcement office carries fees paid by the debtor.
  - Risk Merkezi updates are made by the seller bank and the VYŞ.
  — [Boğaziçi VYŞ SSS](https://bogazicivarlik.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/) (company page; some text appears copied from other VYŞs — low reliability)
- [L-TR] **2021/115 (09.02.2021):** the bank was fined **175,000 TL** after the debtor's sister's number was stored as an "other phone" in the legal-follow-up system and received SMS and calls. There was no legal basis to collect or share it. — [KVKK 2021/115](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/6928/2021-115)
- [L-TR] **2022/184 (04.03.2022):** a debt-collection company saved the numbers of people who called it as the debtor's numbers and shared debt information with them. It was fined **50,000 TL**. — [KVKK 2022/184](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7295/2022-184)
- [L-TR] **2022/1281:** contacting a debtor's son was found lawful only because he was a party to the seizure (appointed custodian, yediemin). This is a narrow, case-specific exception. — [KVKK 2022/1281](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/7589/2022-1281)
- [L-TR] A commercial practice is "aggressive" if harassment, coercion or undue influence significantly impairs the consumer's freedom of choice. Relevant factors: timing, place, nature and **persistence**; threats or insults; exploiting misfortune; disproportionate obstacles; threats of unlawful action (Ticari Reklam ve Haksız Ticari Uygulamalar Yön. art. 31). — [Yönetmelik](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/mevzuat?MevzuatNo=20435&MevzuatTur=7&MevzuatTertip=5) [V: how it applies to bank collections]
- **Customer phrasings (anecdotal):**
  - "Yasal takibe düşmeden borcumu kapatabilmek için… yapılandırmak istedim… talebim 'uygun bulunmamıştır'" — [Şikayetvar](https://www.sikayetvar.com/kredi-karti-yapilandirma)
  - "yalnızca 16 günlük bir gecikme olmasına rağmen… reddediyor" — [Şikayetvar/Garanti](https://www.sikayetvar.com/garanti-bbva/kredi-karti-yapilandirma/yasal-takip)
  - "Düzenli Gelire Rağmen Borç Yapılandırma Talebim Reddedildi" — [Şikayetvar/Yapı Kredi](https://www.sikayetvar.com/yapi-kredi-bankasi/kredi/borc-yapilandirma?page=2)

**UK — FCA CONC 7 (binding; current Handbook text)**
- [L-UK] Forbearance and arrears:
  - Customers in or **approaching** arrears (they say they are at risk) must be treated with **forbearance and due consideration**, taking their individual circumstances into account (CONC 7.3.4R).
  - Examples (7.3.5G): suspending, reducing or waiving interest and charges; deferring arrears; accepting reduced or token payments for a reasonable period; repayment arrangements; refinancing.
  - Arrangements must be **sustainable**, i.e., leave room for priority debts and essential living expenses: mortgage or rent, council tax, food, utilities.
  - Income and expenditure assessments must be **objective**; the Standard Financial Statement (SFS) may be used.
  - While a customer meets an arrangement, interest and charges must be reduced or waived so that **the debt does not rise**.
  - Forbearance must be reviewed.
  — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)
- [L-UK] Debt advice and pressure:
  - Firms should tell customers about free, impartial debt advice and refer them (e.g., MoneyHelper), and share the I&E record.
  - They must not refuse to negotiate.
  - They must not pressurise customers to pay within an unreasonably short period, or to sell property, borrow or take a pension lump sum (7.3.10R).
  - They must **suspend active recovery for a reasonable period (generally 30 days, consider +30)** while a repayment plan is being developed (7.3.11R).
  — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)
- [L-UK] Information, proportionality and charges:
  - Firms *should* (guidance) give customers information that covers how the debt and any forbearance are **reported to the credit file**.
  - No disproportionate action. Repossession only as a last resort.
  - No threats of court action to pressurise customers into paying more than they can afford.
  - Default charges no higher than reasonable costs (7.7).
  — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)
- [L-UK] Contact and third parties (CONC 7.9):
  - Say who you are, your role and the purpose of the call.
  - No misleading prompts (e.g., a fake "missed delivery" card).
  - No unreasonable contact times; respect requests such as shift-work timing.
  - No premium-rate numbers.
  - No unfair disclosure, or threat of disclosure, to third parties. Take reasonable steps so third parties do not learn of the debt (post marked "private and confidential", no debt-collector name visible).
  - **Do not disclose debt details until the person's identity is established (7.9.10R).**
  — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)
- [L-UK] Mental capacity, disputes and limitation:
  - **Mental capacity** limitations: CONC 7.10; a third party may act under a registered lasting power of attorney.
  - **Disputed debt:** recovery must be suspended when the debt is disputed on valid or possibly valid grounds — wrong person, debt does not exist, wrong amount — and the firm must investigate and give details (7.14).
  - **Statute-barred debt:** limitation is 6 years in England, Wales and Northern Ireland, and 5 years (prescriptive) in Scotland. Firms must not pursue the debt in England, Wales or Northern Ireland if there was no contact during the period, and must not mislead (7.15).
  — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)

A **mental health crisis** breathing space lasts for the treatment plus 30 days. — [GOV.UK guidance for creditors (updated 9 Jun 2023)](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/debt-respite-scheme-breathing-space-guidance/debt-respite-scheme-breathing-space-guidance-for-creditors)
- [I-UK] The **LSB Standards of Lending Practice (personal customers) were retired on 31 Mar 2025**, so they are no longer a current benchmark. — [Regulation Tomorrow](https://www.regulationtomorrow.com/2025/01/lsb-announces-plans-to-retire-its-standards-of-lending-practice-for-personal-customers/); [NatWest](https://www.natwest.com/support-centre/bank-accounts-and-supporting-information/general/what-are-the-standards-of-lending-practice.html). ### Inferences
- **Collection stages for routing [I/R; verify thresholds per bank]:**

  | Stage | Days past due | Approach |
  |---|---|---|
  | Pre-delinquency / "approaching arrears" | Customer signals difficulty | Proactive support; UK treats this as in scope |
  | Early | 1–30 | Reminders, a cause conversation, PTP or catch-up |
  | Mid | 31–90 | Group 2; hardship assessment, restructuring routes; for loans, notice possible after 2 consecutive missed installments |
  | Late | 90+ | Non-performing; legal follow-up through lawyers, icra; possible VYŞ sale; human-only handling |

  The agent's role narrows at each stage. From the legal stage onward it informs and routes; it does not negotiate.
- **Outbound call flow [R]:**
  1. Greeting with bank name and agent identity, AI disclosure as per team 12 policy, no purpose stated yet.
  2. "Am I speaking with {full name}?"
  3. Authenticate with non-secret identifiers, per the authentication policy (team 08). **Never ask for PINs, passwords, CVV or one-time codes.**
  4. State the purpose: overdue {product}.
  5. Ask about the cause and listen. Screen for vulnerability.
  6. Present options: pay now through official channels, a PTP, a hardship referral.
  7. Confirm the PTP.
  8. Mention debt-advice or support resources where relevant.
  9. Summarise and send written confirmation.
  10. Log the call.
  If the person is not the right party: say nothing about the debt or the department. Leave only a name, the bank name and a callback number. Do **not** store the third party's number as a contact for the debtor; that was the KVKK 2021/115 and 2022/184 violation pattern.
- **Inbound call ("borcumu ödeyemiyorum", "taksidimi geciktireceğim").** Authenticate → check arrears status and stage → if the customer is "approaching arrears", offer support before the payment is missed [L-UK benchmark; R for TR] → check affordability → PTP or hardship referral.
- **PTP capture spec [R]:**
  - *Required fields:* exact amount (TL), date (cannot be later than the policy maximum), payment channel, the account or reference to use, the customer's confirmation in their own words, consequence wording (late interest continues; the stage may progress), a reminder consent, the agent ID and the call recording ID.
  - *Readback (TR):* "Özetliyorum: {tarih} tarihinde {kanal} üzerinden {tutar} TL ödeme yapacağınızı belirttiniz. Bu ödeme gecikmedeki {gecikme_tutarı} TL'nin {tamamını/bir kısmını} karşılar. Ödeme gecikirse gecikme faizi işlemeye devam eder. Bu bilgiyi SMS ile de iletiyorum; uygun mu?"
  - *Broken promise:* one courtesy follow-up. Ask what changed. Do not threaten. Reassess affordability. After repeated broken promises, route to a human or hardship review per policy.
- **Hardship and forbearance menu (what the agent may describe, not grant):**
  - extension / restructuring within the BDDK maturity limits
  - a payment holiday or deferral (bank-specific)
  - a temporary reduced payment
  - consolidation (e.g., [B: Garanti] "İç Borç" / "Borç Kapatma Kredisi")
  - settlement at VYŞ stage.
  All are **subject to approval**. In the UK an objective income–expenditure assessment (SFS) underpins decisions; in Türkiye, document-based income checks are increasingly required (BDDK 2026) [L-UK; L-TR].
- **Vulnerability in collections [R, built on the GOV.UK toolkit and CONC 7.10]:**
  - Respond to a disclosure with TEXAS.
  - Explicit consent before recording health data [V: KVKK special-category basis, team 02].
  - Pause the collection objective.
  - Offer a human specialist and a callback at a time the customer chooses.
  - Suicidal disclosure → immediate handoff and emergency procedure per policy (BLAKE). The AI agent must never continue the collection script.
  - *Wording (TR):* "Bunu benimle paylaştığınız için teşekkür ederim. Size daha uygun destek verebilmemiz için bu bilgiyi hesabınıza not olarak kaydetmek istiyorum; yalnızca ilgili ekiplerimiz görebilir. Kaydetmeme izin veriyor musunuz?"
- **Third-party and carer handling [R]:** "Hesapla ilgili bilgileri yalnızca hesap sahibiyle paylaşabiliyorum; size bunun sebebini açıklamak isterim…" Follow CARERS: record unverified observations with consent, and explain the authority-to-act route (vekâletname). Never confirm that a debt exists.
- **Disputes ("bu borç benim değil", "tutar yanlış").** Suspend the collection pitch, log the dispute and route it. Identity theft → fraud (team 10). Card items → 5464 objection route (within 10 days of the due date). UK benchmark: suspend recovery while investigating [L-UK]. Never say "you must pay first, then dispute".
- **Legal stage ("yasal takibe düştüm", "icra geldi", "maaşıma haciz geldi").** Confirm the stage from the system, give the file or law-firm contact and payment channels, and do not give legal opinions (zamanaşımı, itiraz süresi). Hand off to the legal/recovery team. *Wording:* "Dosyanız {birim/hukuk bürosu} tarafından yürütülüyor; ödeme ve anlaşma seçenekleri için sizi doğrudan yetkili birime bağlıyorum. Hukuki sonuçlar konusunda kesin bilgi veremem."
- **Prohibited conduct checklist (TR + benchmarks) [L/R]:**
  - threats or insults, or implying criminal liability for an ordinary debt [V for TR criminal-law detail]
  - false urgency or invented deadlines
  - misrepresenting legal consequences
  - contacting relatives, employers or neighbours about the debt
  - calling numbers not verified as the debtor's
  - repeated calls designed to wear the customer down (TR art. 31 "persistence" factor; US 7-in-7 benchmark)
  - calls at unreasonable hours (US 8–21 benchmark; UK "unreasonable times")
  - pressure to borrow or sell assets to pay (UK 7.3.10R)
  - demanding fees not in the contract.
- **Documentation and callbacks [R]:** log date/time, channel, right-party status, verification method, cause of arrears, vulnerability flag (with consent), options discussed, PTP fields, disputes, the customer's contact-time preferences, and the next action with its owner. Callbacks are made at the customer's chosen time. Hand-off triggers to a human: hardship or forbearance decisions, disputes, the legal stage, vulnerability, anger or complaint escalation, any request for a settlement discount, and the customer asking for a human.
- **Never promise (collections):** stopping legal action, a settlement discount, approval of restructuring, deletion of KKB records, waiver of interest or fees, "no impact on your credit score", or "legal right to restructure".

### Gaps
- No Turkish rule found on collection call hours, frequency, voicemail or SMS content. The BDDK call-centre regulation (MevzuatNo 34535) and the accessibility regulation (38873) were not reviewed [V].
- How the BDDK support-services (outsourcing) regulation applies to external collection agencies was not reviewed.
- The Turkish statute of limitations for loan and card debts was not researched (team 02).
- Bank set-off from deposit or salary accounts ("virman", "hesabıma bloke") is a frequent complaint, but its rules were not researched.

## 8. Automatic repayment failures, late-payment charges and grace periods

### Takeaway
In Türkiye a missed installment triggers late interest from the first day late, calculated on the principal portion, capped at contractual rate + 30%, plus taxes. There is no separate statutory late fee for consumer loans in the fee rules reviewed. For cards, an unpaid minimum may attract only late interest. Auto-debit behaviour when there are insufficient funds (retries, partial collection, covering from the KMH) is bank policy. The agent must read the actual schedule and the transaction log.

### Cited Findings
- [L-TR] Late or defaulted installments: late interest on the **principal portion** × days late at ≤ contractual rate + 30%, plus taxes, computed from the actual payment date (TKS Yön. art. 18(2)–(3)). — [TKS Yön.](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/data/5e819a8e13b876a1b04c7a4a/T%C3%BCketici%20Kredisi%20S%C3%B6zle%C5%9Fmeleri%20Y%C3%B6netmeli%C4%9Fi.pdf)
- [L-TR] Cards: if the minimum is unpaid, the cardholder can be charged nothing for the unpaid amount except the contractual late interest, and nothing not in the contract may be charged (5464 art. 24). — [5464](https://www.mevzuat.gov.tr/MevzuatMetin/1.5.5464.pdf)
- [L-TR] No fee for changing the payment plan. Any consumer-loan fee other than the allocation fee is barred (Tebliğ art. 10(1)). A new plan after ara ödeme is free (TKS Yön. art. 17(5)). — [Tebliğ](https://mevzuat.gov.tr/File/GeneratePdf?mevzuatNo=34343&mevzuatTur=Teblig&mevzuatTertip=5)
- [L-TR] A loan cannot be tied to a non-credit product. The Ministry lists **automatic payment instruction** among side products that cannot be made a condition of the loan. — [Ticaret Bakanlığı](https://tuketici.ticaret.gov.tr/yayinlar/tuketici-bilgi-rehberi/tuketici-kredisi-sozlesmeleri-hakkinda-bilgilendirme)
- [B: Garanti BBVA] Installment-KMH installments are collected automatically from the linked current account on the installment date. If the balance is short, all or part is covered from the KMH limit, without interest charged on the interest included. Loan installments can be paid automatically or through the app, web or branch. — [Garanti SSS](https://www.garantibbva.com.tr/sikca-sorulan-sorular/krediler)
- [B: Kuveyt Türk] A card debt left unpaid by the due date is debited automatically from the account (search-result extract only). — [Kuveyt Türk Sağlam Kart SSS](https://saglamkart.kuveytturk.com.tr/sss). [B: QNB] Standing-instruction dates cannot be set on weekends or holidays. — [QNB SSS Ödemeler](https://www.qnb.com.tr/sss/odemeler)
- [L-UK] For continuous payment authority (CPA) failures: if the full amount is not collected by the end of the next working day, this indicates possible financial difficulty. The firm should suspend the CPA, make reasonable efforts to contact the customer, and stop if the customer has evidenced difficulty (CONC 7.6). — [FCA CONC 7](https://handbook.fca.org.uk/handbook/conc7)
- [L-US] The CFPB $8 credit-card late-fee rule was **vacated on 15 Apr 2025** (N.D. Tex.). — [CFPB penalty fees page](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/compliance/compliance-resources/consumer-cards-resources/credit-card-penalty-fees/); [Holland & Knight](https://www.hklaw.com/en/insights/publications/2025/04/cfpb-credit-card-late-fees-rule-vacated-by-texas-district-court)

### Inferences
- **Playbook J — "Talimatım vardı ama taksit çekilmemiş / gecikmeye düşmüşüm" ("I had an instruction but the installment wasn't collected / I've gone into arrears").**
  - *Determine:* whether the instruction existed and on which account; the balance on the due date; any partial collection; any account change (closed account, new IBAN, blocked account); whether the due date fell on a holiday.
  - *Check:* the debit attempt log, the days late, the late interest charged (principal × rate × days, at most contractual rate + 30%), and the KKB impact window.
  - *Options:* pay the overdue amount now through an official channel; repair or re-set the instruction; if the bank's error is proven, request reversal of late interest and correction of the credit record.
  - *Never:* say "it won't affect your record" without checking.
  - *Escalate:* to payments operations (team 07) if a system failure is suspected; to credit reporting if the record was wrongly updated.
  - *Wording (TR):* "Talimatınız {hesap} hesabından {tarih}'te tahsil denemesi yapmış, ancak bakiye yetersiz olduğu için tahsilat gerçekleşmemiş. Bugün ödemeniz halinde {gün} günlük gecikme faizi ({hesap}: anapara × %{oran} × gün/30) ve vergileri eklenir. Talimatınızı güncellemek ister misiniz?"
- [R] Do not quote "grace days" unless the bank publishes them. In Türkiye the statutory card "grace" is the ≥10-day statement-to-due-date gap, not a post-due-date tolerance.

### Gaps
- Turkish banks' retry rules for failed auto-debits (same day, next days, partial collection) are not public in the sources reviewed.
- Whether any bank charges a separate "gecikme ücreti" or notary costs on consumer loans needs product-level confirmation [V].
